The Mind pillar

The story of Mind

Mind is the pillar of recovery, and the one that keeps the whole journey alive. Its objective is simple: help people recover well and stay with the program, so their metabolism keeps improving. This one-pager walks through the concept, the science, a glimpse of the experience, and the math behind the score.

The insight

The belief we need to break.

More sleep is the fix.

It isn't, at least not on its own. What protects your metabolism is not how long you sleep, it is how steady your clock is. Sleep and wake at wildly different times and your body never settles, even after a long night. The regularity of your sleep matters as much as the amount.

The concept

Rhythm

"Sleeping more isn't the answer.
Sleeping steady is."

Rhythm is not about one good night. It is a lens on your body's clock, and it reads three things together:

  • When you sleep and wake, your timing
  • How long you sleep, your hours
  • The wind-down routine that gets you there

How it works

What builds your Rhythm.

  • 1
    Wind-down, the coach's routine
    The nightly sleep-hygiene actions the success coach assigns: no late caffeine, screens off before bed, bed only for sleep, a relaxing wind-down. Checked off each night.
  • 2
    Consistency, the moat
    How close your sleep and wake times land to your usual window. The regularity signal, and the one no other app scores.
  • 3
    Sleep, the baseline
    How long you slept against your goal. It matters, just not as the hero, because steady beats long.
The logic

The math, in one line.

Rhythm = Wind-down (40) + Sleep (20) + Consistency (40)

Robustness

Why it holds up.

  • ✓
    It rewards steadiness, not just hours. A long but erratic night cannot win. A steady, slightly short night can. That is the concept built into the math.
  • ✓
    It runs on data you already have. Sleep timing and duration come from Apple Health and Google Fit. No ring required.
  • ✓
    The coach's recovery routine is in the score. Her wind-down tools count. Her cognitive and motivation tools stay a voluntary safety net, never gamified, because grading them would break them.
  • ✓
    Regularity is the ownable signal. Everyone else shows sleep hours. Consistency is the differentiator no other app scores.
