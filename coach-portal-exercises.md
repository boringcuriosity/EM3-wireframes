# Coach portal exercises

Every exercise a coach can pick for a routine in the coach portal's exercise template
(coach.mytatva.in/exercise-template → Assign Exercise Plan → a routine row's exercise dropdown).
Read on 22 Oct 2026 from the live dropdown, with Disease set to Diabetes. Nothing was changed or saved.

**Total: 202** (201 real, plus one test entry, "testing video 200").

These are the coach's guided exercises, each with its own video, reps and sets. They are a different list
from the 103 workouts a user can log themselves on Move (Walking, Swimming, Badminton…, from the live
app's Select Exercise sheet). The two lists share almost no names.

## Notes from reading it

- The dropdown has no groups, but it runs A to Z twice: the first 24 (ACBT … Yoga Breathing essential) are
  sorted on their own, then the rest start again at "Abs - 2". They are mostly breathing and mobility, so
  they may come from a separate source or type in the backend.
- Likely duplicates or typos worth cleaning up in the backend:
  - "High Knee" and "High Knees"
  - "Arm Circle - NAFLD" and "Arm circles"; "Scapular Retraction" and "Scapular Retraction - NAFLD"
  - "Pilate s- 4" (should be "Pilates - 4"), "Leg Workour-3" (Workout), "Quatrus lumborum stretch" (Quadratus)
  - "Lateral Coastal Breathing" (Costal)
  - Inconsistent spacing in series names: "Beginner Endurance -2" / "Beginner Endurance- 5" / "Beginner Endurance-1"
  - "testing video 200" is a test entry
- Many are numbered series rather than single moves: Beginner Endurance 1–5, Belly Burn 1–6, Core conditioning 1–6,
  Core Yoga 1–4, Dance Workout 1–6, Ergonomic 1–6, Fat Loss 1–3, HIIT 1–7, Lean muscle development 1–6,
  Leg Workout 1–3, Pilates 1–6, Strength and conditioning 1–5, Yoga for Back Pain 1–2, Menstrual yoga 1–2.
