How it works

Your coach sets your targets

For every meal, your coach plans how much protein, carbs and fat you need. Eat close to those amounts and the meal earns its full share of your sufficiency score.

Your rewards

Earn a medal and FlipCoins

Reach a sufficiency of 80% in a day to earn a Medal. Keep logging your meals to earn FlipCoins to spend on your favourite brands ❤️
