The strip behind the search bar

The camera and the microphone come out of the field. In their place a slim strip sits behind the bar and shows only its lower edge, so it reads as something tucked under rather than a second control bar. It says the two hands free ways in, and it retracts the moment somebody starts typing, because once they are typing they have already chosen.

Why the icons had to go. Two 44px targets inside a 52px field crowd the one thing the field is for, and neither icon on its own says you can log a whole day by talking. A strip has room for words, and words are what make the offer legible.

The test is the retract. Press Type on any phone. A strip that is charming on arrival and clumsy on the way out is worse than no strip, so watch the exit as closely as the entrance.
Or use the controls on each phone.
