Move · Learn

Videos in Learn

Learn becomes one page: the three newest articles with View all, then the videos, holding exactly what the app shows under Exercise today: the exercise of the week, then Breathing, Cardio, Walking, Strength and cardio, and Strength & conditioning, with the same titles, levels, like counts, covers and descriptions. Nothing is added. The phone is the live page.

  • The app's own covers, whole. Every cover is 712 x 233 (3.06:1); the card shows it at that shape, in FlipFeed's sticker edge: an ink hairline and a light indigo offset.
  • Exercise of the week wears the verdict tag's build (the gradient fill and conic hairline of Solid and Great), in Move's indigo, with a star.
  • View all and See all each open a page of their own, with a back arrow: every article, every video in a row, or everything saved.
  • Each thing once. Minutes on the cover, then the title, then level and equipment, then two lines of the description.
  • Know more sits inside the second line, over a soft fade, only when the words run longer. It opens a sheet with the whole description: what it is, then Steps to perform, Do's and Don'ts where the app has them (Mountain climber, High knee), and Play video at the foot. The info icon goes.
  • Play: a tap on a cover opens the video full screen, turned to landscape. The shrink button turns it back to portrait, with the whole description under the video; X closes from either. Play and pause, back and forward 10 seconds, a bar to drag. Built on the routine player, so both look and turn the same. Its floating Up next band plays on through the row the video came from, with See all.
  • Bookmark on the cover, top right. Saving shows a short note and adds a Saved row under the exercise of the week, the only place to find them again (the web app has none today).
  • Heart by the title. It likes or unlikes for you; the number is everyone's likes, hidden under 10.

What we kept from the research

  • Mobbin (Fitbit Premium, Apple Fitness+, Open, Tonal): rows with the next card peeking; nothing on the picture but the time and a save; title and one quiet line below.
  • GoodFlip Figma (FlipFeed, GF Content and Community): there is no video card in the system yet, so the cards borrow FlipFeed's section head (600 20/26), its rail spacing, and for Sticker its ink edge.
  • GoodFlip code (app.goodflip.in, MyTatva-RN, MyTatvaCore): what the heart and bookmark do today, and the duration field. Notes: notes/move-videos-like-bookmark.md.
  • Colour: white page, green for actions (See all, Know more, a saved bookmark), a warm red only on a liked heart.
