Move · Log exercise

How long did you do it?

Five live ways to pick a duration from 5 to 120 minutes in 5 minute steps. Each card is the top of the workout screen; drag, scroll and tap to feel it. All start at 5 min, as the screen does.

ARuler with quick picksRECOMMENDED

A big green number over a thin ruler that snaps every 5 minutes; longer ticks at every 15. Four chips jump to the usual answers.

Why: calm, one hand, the number leads. Chips make the common case one tap and save an overshot flick.

Watch: needs a slider role for VoiceOver (5 min per swipe).

Seen in Me+, TIDE, Brink
BQuick picks first

Six large tiles for the usual answers; Other opens the ruler in place for anything else.

Why: the fastest for most logs and the most accessible, they are plain buttons.

Watch: 75 or 105 minutes takes a second step.

Seen in Apple Fitness, Cal AI, BitePal
CDial that fills

Drag round the ring and it fills green, like a clock face; the full ring is 2 hours.

Why: the most memorable, reads as time with no words.

Watch: a circle is awkward one handed and fiddly on small phones; the hardest to make accessible.

Seen in Tiimo, Airbnb
DBig number, drag or tap

The number is the control: drag it up or down, or tap minus and plus. The values either side peek in faintly.

Why: playful and very clear; the buttons give an exact, accessible fallback.

Watch: dragging is not obvious without the hint; 24 steps is a lot of tapping.

Seen in Any Distance, Opal
EFill bar

One wide bar in 24 segments that fills green as you drag or tap along it.

Why: shows how much of 2 hours you did at a glance; easy to hit with a thumb.

Watch: each 5 minute step is a narrow target on small phones.

Seen in Nike Run Club (effort)
