The plate: making the dots read as beads, and what the plate can do

Two decisions, kept apart because they are separate. The top half is how a dot is drawn, which costs nothing per frame and is the change that actually produces the three dimensional read. The bottom half is what the plate does, which is where the frame budget goes. Every plate below is live and drawn by the same painter, so the only thing that differs between two cards is the thing the card is named after.

Sizes, ring radii, dot counts, the random seed and the palette are lifted from r3/heroes/plate.js unchanged, so these are the real dots and not a mock-up of them. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion; turn it on and every card below settles immediately into its finished state.

1. How a dot is drawn

All four cards are the same day at 88. Nothing moves that is not moving in all of them. The magnified strip under each one is the same dot at six times size, because a decision about a 10px bead cannot honestly be made at 10px.

2. What the plate does

Each card uses the bead from card D above, so the motion is the only variable. Press Replay to see the move again.

3. The two states seen every day

Loading and the empty morning are the states a user meets most often, which makes them the ones most able to grate. Both of these are repairs to something already in the file rather than additions.
