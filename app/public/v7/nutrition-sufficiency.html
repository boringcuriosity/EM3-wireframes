    GoodFlip's home screen, built from the design system's own Homepage recipe and its components (top nav, welcome strip, banner carousel, score card, services, trackers, bottom nav). Under the carousel sits Your EM3: one card per pillar, scrolled sideways, each carrying the pillar's pictogram, its score's name, and the score in the bowl of a rising arc of that pillar's orbs.     Section 3

    The score Sufficient

    • One number, nutrition sufficiency out of 100, the mean of three capped ratios: protein, carbs and fats against the coach's targets.
    • It counts up from zero when the screen opens, over about 1.2 seconds.
    • The plate carries the number and the word SUFFICIENT only. No meal counts, no dates, no verdicts.
    • Green, because the score is the one thing that carries the pillar's colour. Everything else may use the wider palette.

    How it is worked out: sufficiency = protein, carbs and fats, each capped at its target, then averaged. The cap is why extra rice cannot fill in for missing protein.

    Section

    Score sandbox

    The score's logic, live: sufficiency-sandbox.html. Log food meal by meal, see every step of the calculation, and watch the nutrition sufficiency score move.

    The sandbox sufficiency-sandbox.html Three columns: log the food, how it is calculated, the score. Open the sandbox

    What it shows

    • Each meal has its own targets, the items the coach planned for it, added up. Its share of the day is its share of the planned calories: breakfast 23%, lunch 28%, dinner 27%.
    • Under a target a macro earns the share reached. A little over (20%, at least 5g) still earns full points. Past that it loses a point for every 1% over, down to −50.
    • Meal points = its share × the average of its three macros. The day adds them up, and never shows below 0.
    • Try a day loads eleven days in one tap: the plan, junk all day, the whole day at breakfast, a banana extra, and more.
    • Settings change the buffer, the penalty and the floor, so a different rule can be tried before it is agreed.

    Section

    The line under the score

    After every log, one short line in a quote card under the score says what that log did, with a way to ask KAIRA why. It waits as a soft shimmer while the score lands, then the words sweep in from the left. The phones in every case on this page carry it too.

    • Went up. "Your score went up by +N%, keep logging your meals. Learn more by asking KAIRA", the number in a green tag.
    • Went up by less than it could. "Your score went up by only +N%, you could have gained N% more. Find out why from KAIRA", the number in an amber tag.
    • Did not move, or dropped. The same line with 0 or −N, and Find out why from KAIRA.
    • Not started. "Log your first meal to see your nutrition sufficiency score grow." No link.
    • KAIRA opens her chat with the question already asked: what was eaten, by name, against the Eat plan suggested by your coach, and the macros that resulted.
    • Not shown where there is no score: no plan yet, a free user, a day with nothing logged.
    Section 17

    A free user

    Someone who uses the app without a care program. There is no coach and no Eat plan, so there is no sufficiency score at all, not even a locked one. The screen is a food diary: log meals, and see protein, carbs, fats and calories add up through the day.

    • No score. The score and everything about it stands down. The day starts at the date, then the macros.
    • Macros in grams. The same three tiles, with what was eaten and no target, so there is nothing to fill towards.
    • Calories as a total. The day's sum, with no goal beside it.
    • Meal cards with no plan. Each meal is a slot to log into, with + on the card. Foods appear as they are logged, without the Manual tag, because everything here is logged by hand.
    • KAIRA offers the two ways to log, and never mentions a coach.
    Section 5

    Meal cards

    • Always open, with no expand, no collapse and no chevron.
    • Header: meal name, "Suggested 8:00 - 10:00 AM · 486 of 382 cal", and a + button.
    • Body: "YOUR COACH'S PLAN", option chips with a check on the chosen one, then food rows with a tick circle, name, quantity, calories and a ⋮ menu. A logged food's tick opens the same menu (change the quantity, take it off your day).
    • Food logged outside the plan sits above the plan with a "Manual" tag.
    • Ticking a coach's food drops it into a floating bar at the bottom: the foods, "Edit quantity & time", Log, and an X. Foods from several meals can go in together, each with its own time. While a meal's foods are in the bar, its other options are locked: one option per meal.
    • A time still to come cannot be logged. If any food in the bar is set later than now (dinner ticked at 1 PM), Log opens the review sheet instead: the late times turn amber, a note asks when they were eaten with a one-tap "Set them to now", and Log waits until every time has passed. On the time wheel, times still to come are greyed and Done waits.
    • "TIPS FROM YOUR COACH" holds the parts of a plan that are not food, each with a tick and a one line tip.
    • A coach's food has three states: logged, not yet, or skipped. Skip lives in the ⋮ menu on each food: the name is struck through, the food stays on the plan and counts for nothing, and a tick or "Put it back on the list" brings it back. A coach's food is never deleted.

    • The score animates from the old number to the new one. A day with no score shows the count of what went in instead.
    • One line from Kaira, never a recap of the items.

    Section 13

    What the backend needs

    The backend has no score. calories_adherence_patient returns raw grams and calories. Sufficiency is ours to define, and three things have to be built first.

    #What is neededWhy
    1A sufficiency value per day, stored or computed cheaply for a month at a time.The date sheet draws a ring per day. Reading every log row for thirty days to draw a calendar is not a read the app can make.
    2A history row when a plan approval overwrites the calorie goal.Approval writes patient_goal_rel.goal_value in place with no history, so a past day cannot be read against the goal that applied then.
    3A decided day boundary.todays_date() returns the server's local date and its IST shift is commented out, so "today" is not the user's day and not explicitly IST.

    Already there, and used as is

    • Photo and voice are first class log sources, with the attachment kept on the row. Snap and Voice map straight onto them.
    • Kaira's logging endpoints exist, including the one that asks an LLM for a food's nutrition when the food is unknown and saves it back.
    • Meal times are real plan data, per meal division, so "Suggested 8:00 - 10:00 AM" changes when the plan changes.
    • Logging onto an earlier day already passes validation, so the past day flow needs no new endpoint.
    • The adherence endpoint already supports 7D, 30D, 90D and longer, which is what the Trend tab will read.
    One thing to watchA manually logged food that matches the meal's plan is silently relinked by the backend, so the "Manual" tag is not the person's choice alone.
    Section 14

    Rules that hold everywhere

    Bottom sheets

    • Title at the top left, filled circle X at the top right in a 44px target.
    • No drag handle drawn.
    • At most 90% of the screen: the header stays put, the body scrolls.
    • Backdrop 50% black, slide up 300ms.

    Visual

    • Page background is pure white. Cards separate themselves with a subtle border and a soft shadow, not a darker page.
    • Most cards use that quiet treatment. A few earn creative depth: Kaira's card carries her indigo to teal gradient stroke with a squared top left corner, primary buttons sit on a solid depth bar, and the date sheet's calendar sits on a bound paper pad.
    • The score carries the pillar colour. Other elements may use the wider GoodFlip palette.
    • Macros are one green, identified by their own icons, never by a colour each.

    Copy

    • Plain, friendly, second person. No em dashes. One "!" at most, and only for a real win.
    • Kaira gives insight, never a recap of what was logged. Two short sentences at most.
    • Nothing under 12px, every tap target at least 44px.
    Where the other colours goIndigo belongs to Kaira and to anything she does. Gold marks insight and reward. Teal is a calm accent. Green stays the score and the primary action.
