    A free user

    Someone who uses the app without a care program. There is no coach and no move plan, so there is no Momentum score at all, not even a locked one, and nothing that hints at one. The screen is a workout diary: log what you did, and see the calories and steps add up through the day.

    • No score. The score and everything about it stands down. The day starts at the date, then the numbers.
    • Workout and Steps. Two tiles with what was done and no target. No Small moves tile, since only a coach sets those.
    • Steps. Counted by their phone, with no goal beside them.
    • Part-of-day cards. Morning to night, each a plain card with a plus to log into. Workouts appear as they are logged.
    • Nothing logged yet. The same screen with 0 kcal, since steps still come in from the phone.
    • After a log. "Workout logged. It adds to what you moved today." Never a coach, never a score.

    The Learn tab

    One page: the three newest articles, then videos. Everything in Videos is what the app shows under Exercise today: the exercise of the week, then Breathing, Cardio, Walking, Strength and cardio, and Strength & conditioning, with the same titles, levels, like counts, covers and descriptions. The fuller write-up, with research: Videos in Learn and the card kit.

    • No page title. The tab bar already says Learn; the page opens on Articles, over the pale indigo fade.
    • Articles. The newest three, View all for the rest.
    • The video card. The app's own cover, whole at 712 x 233, with an indigo stroke and a light indigo offset. Minutes on the cover, then the title with the heart, then level and equipment, then two lines of the description.
    • Exercise of the week. The same card at full width, with a green tag built like the Solid and Great tags.
    • Know more. Inside the second line, only when the words run longer. It opens a sheet with all of it: Steps to perform, Do's and Don'ts where the app has them (Mountain climber, High knee), and Play video.
    • Heart. Likes or unlikes for you; the number is everyone's, hidden under 10. A press swells it a little and breathes a faint ring out once.
    • Bookmark. Small dark glass on the cover. Saved fills the mark white, with the same swell and ring, and a short note. A Saved row appears under the exercise of the week. Today the web app saves with nowhere to see them again; the native app keeps them in a Bookmarks screen in its side menu.
    • See all and View all. Each opens a page of its own with a back arrow: every video in a row, every article, or everything saved. The Saved row only exists once something is saved, so there is no empty state.
    • Play. Full screen, turned to landscape; the shrink button turns it back to portrait with the description under it; X closes. Play, 10 seconds back and forward, a bar to drag.
    • Up next. In portrait, the routine player's floating band shows the next video in the row it was opened from (Breathing plays on through Breathing, Saved through Saved), with See all to open the row. The last one says so rather than looping; the exercise of the week stands alone, so it has none. In landscape, the end offers Watch again and Next video.
    • Placeholders. Minutes are samples until the app reads time_duration, and the player plays a sample clip: the app's video links are signed and are not copied.
