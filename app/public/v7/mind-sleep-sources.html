Where last night's sleep comes from

Three ways sleep reaches the Mind screen: the person types it, Health Connect brings it in from a watch or sleep app, or the Arc smart ring writes it to Health Connect and we read it from there. GoodFlip never reads the ring directly. Health Connect sits in the middle, so there is one permission and one path. Every frame below is the real Mind screen.

ManualThe person types when they slept and woke up, from Log sleep on the card. Always there, whatever else is connected.
Health ConnectA watch or sleep app writes sleep to Health Connect, and GoodFlip reads it once allowed. Updated each morning; the person can still edit the times.
Arc ring, via Health ConnectThe Arc app writes the ring's sleep to Health Connect. GoodFlip asks for Health Connect as it does for anyone, and the strip names both.

The flows

No ring: two options, kept simple

  1. The sleep card offers two things: Log sleep, and the strip under it, Turn on Health Connect.
  2. Turn on Health Connect opens our sheet ("Let your phone fill in your sleep"), then Android's own Health Connect permission screen.
  3. Allowed: sleep comes in each morning. The strip reads "From Health Connect · Updated 7:22 AM".
  4. Not allowed or Not now: the person types last night. The strip keeps offering Health Connect, never a nag elsewhere.

With the Arc ring: same permission, the ring named

  1. The ring is paired in the Arc app, which writes sleep to Health Connect.
  2. GoodFlip still asks for Health Connect. The sheet says it plainly: "Let your Arc ring fill in your sleep".
  3. Allowed: the ring's sleep comes in through Health Connect. The strip shows the ring and Health Connect together: "From Arc ring via Health Connect · 7:22 AM".
  4. Not allowed: sleep is typed, as with no ring. The strip says "Turn on Health Connect · Your Arc ring's sleep fills in by itself".

The ring mark in the strip is the Arc ring image (icons/mind/arc-ring.png), set in the same small white tile as the Health Connect mark (RINGI in mind/variants/sleep.js).

Open questions

  1. Ring strip words: "From Arc ring via Health Connect" (A, says how it travels) or "Arc ring · Health Connect" (B, shorter, fits beside "Updated 7:22 AM"). A is on the screen now.
  2. How do we know someone has the ring? From the order, the Arc app being installed, or the Arc app's name on the sleep records in Health Connect? The screen needs a yes or no before it says "Arc ring".
  3. When the ring's sleep and a watch's sleep both reach Health Connect for the same night, which wins? Suggest: the ring when present.
  4. Should a ring owner who denies Health Connect see one nudge after a few typed nights, or only the strip?
  5. iPhone: the same three, with Apple Health in place of Health Connect, when the Arc app writes to Apple Health.
