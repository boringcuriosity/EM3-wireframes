Sets, reps and rest that anyone can follow

Today the player shows four chips under the move: 2 sets, 10 reps, 30 sec between sets, 15 sec before next. They are accurate, but a first-time user has to know what a set and a rep are, and work out how the two rests fit together. Every option below is the same Plank to push up, drawn in the player, with the same numbers.

What the research says. No leading fitness app shows rest as a chip beside sets and reps. Google Health (Fitbit), Tonal and Bevel turn each set and each rest into a step in order; Peloton and Centr show one number at a time while you move; Hevy, Peloton and Ladder start the rest timer for you and show what comes next. The NHS writes "repeat 5 times" for older adults and keeps "sets" for the hard version. So the words that work are times and rounds, and the rest is something the app runs, not something to work out.

Mobbin, what top apps do

  • Google Health: the session unrolled, "Set 1 · 12 reps", then "Rest 30 secs" as its own row. screen
  • Fitbit and Ladder: rest as a full screen countdown with "Next: Set 2 of 3". screen screen
  • Peloton Strength+: "Set 1 / 1" and a big rep count while you move; "3x · 8 reps" in the list. screen
  • Runna: one sentence, "Do one set, rest 90 seconds and repeat 2 times", then set cards. screen
  • Tonal: "Rest, 30 secs" as a list row between moves. screen

The web, plain words and timers

  • NHS strength exercises for older people: "Repeat five times", sets only in the hard version. nhs.uk
  • Fitbod explains it in help only: "do it 10 times in a row, rest, repeat". help.fitbod.me
  • Hevy starts rest on its own when a set is done, with +15 and Skip. hevyapp.com
  • Seven counts the last 10 seconds of rest out loud and names the next move. applevis.com
  • Older adults misread handouts with complex wording; a spoken guide does as well as video. study

GoodFlip system, how it is drawn

  • Brand green for the move being done, greys for rest, indigo or gold only for a note: tokens from r3/goodflip-ui.css.
  • Things you cannot tap stay flat (no hard offset shadow); chips are for tapping, so static facts become tiles or a timeline.
  • The system's vertical stepper (green done, ringed current, hollow upcoming) is the base for the checklist option.
  • No timer component exists yet; the rest ring is new, built from the circular progress ring.
