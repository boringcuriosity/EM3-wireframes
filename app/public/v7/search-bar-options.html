The search bar, softened

The field and the strip are one object now, so the field should carry the same care. Six treatments of the same bar, each shown with the strip under it, because the pair is what you are really judging. Press Focus on any phone to see the focused state, which is where a soft bar most often falls apart.

The trap with a shadow on a search field. A field is a place to put something into, so it wants to read as recessed. A drop shadow says raised. Most of the beautiful versions below resolve that by keeping the shadow very soft and very close, so it reads as the card lifting off the page rather than the field lifting off the card.

And the strip has to keep working. It tucks 22px behind the bar, so anything the bar gains at its bottom edge, a shadow included, lands on the strip. Watch that seam.
