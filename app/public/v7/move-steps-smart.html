The steps card, with the smart strip

The steps card stays exactly as it is. Only the strip under it changes, to the same pattern as Mind's sleep card: always tappable, the time at the left with the source's icon, one action at the right end. Every phone is the real Move screen; tap the strip in any of them.

How it decides

  1. The ring first. If the Arc smart ring is paired, steps come from it. It syncs inside GoodFlip.
  2. Then Apple Health. If Apple Health is allowed, steps come from it.
  3. Else they log it. Log steps on the card works as today; what they type says Added by you.
  4. Asked once. The first time on Move, Apple Health is asked for once, as today. After that, connecting lives in the strip.

1. The strip under the steps card

  • Nothing connected, nothing logged: Track steps automatically, with a chevron.
  • Logged by hand, nothing connected: Added by you, 9:00 AM, with Connect.
  • Syncing: Syncing with Apple Health (or your Arc ring), no action until it is done.
  • Synced: Last synced 3:38 PM, with Manage.
  • Connected, no steps came: No steps data found · 3:38 PM, with Manage.

With a device connected, Move opens syncing for about 2.5 s, then the steps arrive. Sync in the sheet does the same, then the toast: Your steps are updated.

The three dots

With a device connected, the card's Log steps becomes three dots. Inside: Log steps, and Track steps automatically. With nothing connected, Log steps stays on the card as today.

2. Track your steps automatically

From the strip. Two rows in one soft card. Not connected: Connect Arc smart ring, Counts steps while you wear it; Connect Apple Health, Reads steps from other apps or devices; each with Connect. Connected: its name, a green dot with Last synced, and Sync.
