The Apple Health sheet

The sheet that asks to read steps from Apple Health, shown before the phone's own permission screen. Three ways, each over the Move screen. Tap a phone to play it again.

Where it opens. From the steps card while Health is not connected, and once on the first visit to Move. After Connect, the phone's own permission screen follows. If they say no there, the steps card keeps Log steps and a quiet line to connect later.
