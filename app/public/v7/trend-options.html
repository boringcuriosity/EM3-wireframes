Trend options

A redesign of the Eat pillar's Trend tab. KAIRA moves to the top and holds her weekly insight until the week is done, then hands it over with a small moment. The chart drops the wall of green and draws each day the way the day view draws it. Daily averages are gone. Every phone below runs on the same week: five days logged at 70, 82, 60, 75 and 83, two not logged, a week score of 74 (the mean of the logged days) and 68 the week before.

The phones are live. Tap any logged day to open its score in a sheet (the real liquid gauge from the day view). Press Play under a phone to run its unlock, or tap Unlock my insight inside it.

1. What is wrong today

The current tab, in its second complete week (r3/trend.html?state=w2).

  1. Everything is green. The bars, the headline number, the verdict chip, the target line and the delta all share one green, so nothing stands out and green stops meaning anything.
  2. Daily averages repeat the day view. Four more gauges below the chart, read on a page that is meant to be about the week. Removed.
  3. KAIRA is at the bottom. Her read is the reason to open a week, and it sits below the fold after the numbers.
  4. Nothing marks a finished week. Seven days of logging end in silence; the page looks the same on day 7 as on day 6.
  5. The chart does not look like the day view. A day is a gauge of orbs on the Eat tab and a green bar here, so a user cannot connect Tuesday here to the Tuesday they saw.
  6. It is monotonous. Card after card of the same shape and weight, with no focal point.

2. The flow

One week, start to finish. KAIRA is always the first card. The chart under her is chart C from section 3, and the unlock is Orbs gather from section 4. The week selector and the bottom nav are unchanged.

3. How each day relates to the gauge

The day view shows a score as a liquid bubble inside an arc of orbs, filled from 0 to 100, with a status tag under it. Here the same seven days need to read as seven of those, and still compare at a glance.

Should it be neutral? Yes. The forms that carry the data (bars, rings, lines) go grey. Colour appears only where it means something: each day's status (green for Solid Day, amber for Room To Grow, red for Needs Attention), drawn as the day view's own orb. The pillar green is spent on one thing, the week's score. The 70 line is where a day becomes Solid, so the line, the orbs and the tags all say the same thing.

4. The unlock

What happens when the user taps Unlock my insight. Each one takes between 2 and 2.6 seconds, uses smooth eases only (no springs or bounce), and ends on a calm, light card. With reduced motion turned on, the insight simply fades in.

Recommendation

  • Flow: KAIRA's card always on top. While the week fills, she says when her insight unlocks and shows the week filling as seven segments. On Monday the card glows and offers Unlock my insight. After the unlock, her insight stays on top and the chart sits under it. Daily averages are removed.
  • Chart: C, Tubes with a status orb. Every day is a 0 to 100 tube on the same scale as the day view's arc, filled in grey, capped with the day view's own orb in the day's status colour. Tapping a day opens its real gauge in a sheet.
  • Colour: neutral greys for the data, status colour only on the orbs and tags, the pillar green only on the week's score of 74.
  • Unlock: 2, Orbs gather. The week's five orbs lift off the chart, take on KAIRA's colour and become her insight. It tells the story the feature is about. Seal lift is the fallback if Orbs gather proves costly to build.
  • Copy: "Your insight unlocks on Monday" while filling, "Your insight is ready" with "One thing stood out in your week." once it is done.
