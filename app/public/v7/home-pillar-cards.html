Home pillar cards

Four entry points on the home screen, one card per pillar. Each card carries only three things: the pillar's pictogram, its score, and one small picture of that score in the score's own orbs. They share one visual system: the Figma orbs, one colour per pillar; GoodFlip's pictograms (Nutrition, Movement, Stress, Lab tests); and GoodFlip's tiles with their light indigo edge and lip, the same on every pillar so colour lives only inside the card. Sample day: Eat 67, Move 54, Mind 72, Measure 81. Measure's score has no name yet, so it reads as "Measure".
