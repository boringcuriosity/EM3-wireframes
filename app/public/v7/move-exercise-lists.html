Move · Exercise lists

Coach plan exercises and the log list

Both lists the person can move with: what their coach assigns, and what they log themselves by search. Each has one icon and its calories per minute for a 70 kg person, taken from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities (kcal per minute = MET × 3.5 × 70 ÷ 200). Duplicates are grouped under one name; nothing is deleted.

Intensity is picked by the person when they log, never by the coach. Coach exercises get one only where the Compendium lists the same activity at different efforts: bodyweight sessions (general, high intensity), calisthenics (light, moderate, vigorous), video workouts (moderate, vigorous), HIIT (moderate, vigorous) and aerobic dance (low, high impact). Each level is named by the Compendium’s effort word (or its MET band when it gives none: under 3 light, 3 to 5.9 moderate, 6 and over vigorous). The code the exercise already used is the default. Pilates, yoga, stretching, stairs, punching bag, step-ups and weight training keep one value: their other Compendium codes are a different style, pace or load, not the same activity done harder.

The Search and log tab is the one list people search when they log a workout: every coach exercise and every log activity, with duplicates removed. A numbered series (Lean muscle development 1 to 6, HIIT 1 to 7) is one entry; “- NAFLD” versions join their twin; a coach exercise with a log activity’s name joins that activity.

Where these come from

Coach list: the live exercise library, 202 videos (content_master, ExerciseVideo), one row each. Log list: the team sheet “Activity logging - exercise_with_intensity”, 286 names and 373 intensity rows; names that are the same activity are merged into 143, with their intensities kept.

Coach exercises earn 0 kcal today

The library has no MET or calorie field, and the calorie total skips plan logs (MyTatvaCore Goal_readings_model.js, plan logs are excluded). The numbers here would be new.

“Duration” is one rep, not the video

In the library, time_duration is the time one rep takes. The coach portal works out the plan’s Duration as (reps × one rep + rest post set) × sets + rest post exercise. So “Neck Pain stretches, 26 min” means one rep is stored as 5 min: a data error to fix.

The log list uses a fixed number

exercise_intensity_rel.cal_per_min is one value per intensity, for 70 kg, with no body weight. The numbers here are updated to the 2024 Compendium, so they replace the sheet’s where they differ.
