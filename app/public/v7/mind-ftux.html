Meeting Rhythm for the first time

The bottom sheet that opens over the Mind screen the first time someone sees it. It follows Eat's and Move's pick, Swipe through, and changes with who is looking: a plan user meets the score, the coach's plan and the medal; someone waiting for a plan meets the locked score and what is open now; a free user meets sleep from Health and the breathing exercises. Every phone is live: swipe it, tap through, close it and replay.

What it says, from mind/rhythm.js and coach-mind/mind-plan-defaults.md: Rhythm is out of 100 and starts fresh each morning. Sleep quality is 70: Sleep duration against the coach’s target 25, Sleep consistency (the sleep window) 25, wind-down 20. Self-care is 30: breathing 10, any one me-time task 10, a mood check-in 10. What the coach leaves out sits out, and the rest share the 100. A day at 80% or more earns the Rhythm medal, Lotus at dawn. The step 2 sample is the default plan, 7.5 hours from 10:30 PM to 6:00 AM: 7h 20m from 10:50 PM gives 49, breathing takes it to 59, a mood check-in to 69. Before a plan there is no score; free users have no score.
