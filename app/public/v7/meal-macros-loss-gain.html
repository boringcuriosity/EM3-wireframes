Meal by meal: weight loss vs weight gain

Average meal targets (each meal's middle option) across 16 weight loss and 5 weight gain templates from the coach portal, as the templates come. Water and tea only meals (under 20 kcal) are left out.

Weight lossWeight gain
Partly trueWeight loss: more carbs in the morning?

By grams, no: breakfast 43.6 g vs dinner 41.8 g, and lunch is highest (48.1 g). Only 9 of 16 templates have more carbs at breakfast than dinner.

By share of the meal, yes, a little: carbs are 61% of breakfast calories and fall to 53% at dinner and 45% at bed time.

Not true for dinnerWeight gain: fewer carbs at night?

Dinner has more carbs than breakfast: 64.2 g vs 56.8 g. Only 1 of 5 templates have more carbs at breakfast.

Bed time is low (16 g), but that's true of weight loss too (14.5 g): every template with both meals has fewer carbs at bed time than dinner (10 of 10 loss, 5 of 5 gain).
