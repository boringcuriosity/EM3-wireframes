One card for calories and macros

Today these are two cards: four filling glyphs in one, a flame and a bar in the other. Merging them is not only a layout job, because calories are the sum and the macros are its parts, so a single card has to make that relationship legible rather than presenting five equal facts.

Every variant below is live, flat, and carries no shadow, so it reads as information rather than as something to press. The filling glyph is the one device carried over unchanged. Variants are filled in once the Figma and Mobbin passes land.
