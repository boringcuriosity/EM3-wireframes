Sets and reps, made obvious

The plan for Plank to push up is 2 sets of 10 reps, 30 seconds between sets, and 15 seconds before the next move. In plain words: do 10, rest 30 seconds, do 10 more, then take 15 seconds before the next exercise. Each option below helps a first-time user see or learn exactly that.

The two words, in one line each. A rep is doing the move once: one push up. A set is a run of reps without stopping: 10 push ups in a row. Every option either teaches this, or shows it so clearly that nobody needs to be taught.

How they differ. 1, 3 and 4 show; 2 and 7 say it in everyday words; 5 teaches the words once and keeps them; 6 teaches by doing. My pick is 1 with 5: the dots make the plan countable at a glance, and the one-tap explainer teaches the words for the coach and the rest of the app. Earlier round: move-sets-reps-options.html.
