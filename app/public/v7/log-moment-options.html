Log moments

The win screen after logging, with Rise's content and nothing more: the pill, the gain, one line. Tapping Log meal sweeps colour from the button to the top left, and the button itself lifts out of the tray and becomes the pill. The gain rolls in digit by digit, a sheen passes over, and soft bubbles drift up. On the way out the gain shrinks into the score on the day while the colour sweeps on. Only the fill changes between options, and the Ground row sets what the win sits on: GoodFlip's green, deep green, white, or one of its tints. Tap a phone to play it, and on a phone you will feel the haptics.

The haptics

Tap8 ms, a light tick as the button goes down.
Filled14 ms, a soft thud as the green reaches the top left.
Pill6 ms, the faintest tick as the button settles into the pill.
Success12, 60, 20 ms, a double beat as the last digit of the gain settles.

Android Chrome plays these through the Vibration API. iOS Safari has no web vibration, so the native app would map them to UIImpactFeedbackGenerator light, soft, rigid and the success notification.
