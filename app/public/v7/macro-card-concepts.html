The macro card, ten ways

The same day, the same three glyphs, the same indigo: Protein 43 of 95g, Carbs 121 of 238g, Fats 32 of 63g, 942 of 1,900 kcal. Each card asks a different question of the same numbers: how full, how far to go, what to eat next, or how the plate looks. Fills arrive slowly on load and nothing bounces.
