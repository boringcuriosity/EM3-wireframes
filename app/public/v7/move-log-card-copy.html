Words for the log card

The card with the green drop and Log workout, under What shaped your score? on Move. It is where a patient adds movement they did outside the coach’s plan: a swim, a cricket match, a yoga class, a dance at a wedding, an hour of housework. The coach’s own exercises are ticked on the part-of-day cards below it. Whatever is logged here adds to calories burned and grows Momentum.

It is read in a glance, by someone on a care program scrolling past their score. So the words have one job: make them think “oh, I did something like that” and tap.

What is weak about today’s words. “Went for a walk” leads with the one thing that already counts on its own through steps, so it invites double counting. “A game” could mean a phone game. “Search and log any workout you did” describes the tool and repeats the button, and nothing says this is for movement beyond the plan or what the patient gets back for it.

What others do

Fitness and health apps on Mobbin, looking at the words on their way into logging an activity. The pattern: most say very little and lean on the button; the good ones say where this fits and what you get.

Patterns. Questions and short statements win; imperatives mostly echo the button. Naming two or three concrete activities beats “any workout”. One line, under about ten words, is the norm. Few apps say why to bother, which is the gap GoodFlip can fill with Momentum.

The options

Each window is the real Move screen with those words, moved to the card. Walking off notes whether the words also suit someone who cannot walk (?able=nowalk, which today has its own words: “Did a workout, a swim or a game? Search and log anything you did.”).

The pick

  • Recommended, B1: “Did more than your plan today?” with “Yoga, cricket, a swim, even housework. It all adds to your Momentum.” It says what the card is for, gives familiar examples, and tells them what they get, in two short lines that suit everyone.
  • Runner up, E4: “Anything beyond your plan?” with “Your plan’s exercises are ticked below. Add everything else here.” The clearest about where each kind of movement goes, at the cost of warmth.
  • Runner up, A1: “Moved outside your plan today?” alone. The shortest way to say the card’s purpose, if the card should stay light.
