What every concept is judged against
  • The right thing now. It knows the hour and the plan, and offers one thing first.
  • Answer where you are. The common answer is one tap on Home. A sheet or the pillar opens only when it needs more.
  • EM3 is motivation. The four pillars stay in view, and a log visibly moves one.
  • GoodFlip's look. White page, pillar colours only on what scores, soft orbs and glass, smooth motion that never bounces, KAIRA as the hexagon.
  • KAIRA carries insight. She never narrates the day back; every line says why.

Part 2. Doors and hooks

Part 1 walked through the day one task at a time. These don't. Each is a simpler Home top: Eat, Move and Mind as doors side by side, opened in a tap, with something above or around them that makes a person want to start. Tap a door and the real pillar opens inside the phone.

What these are judged against
  • Three doors at a glance. Eat, Move, Mind (and Measure) are visible together, without scrolling.
  • One tap in. A door opens its pillar, or its most useful action, in one tap.
  • A hook to start. Something gives a reason to open a door now: a streak, an insight, a goal, a reward.
  • GoodFlip's look. White page, pillar colour only on what scores, soft orbs and glass, smooth motion.
  • Calm. It reads in two seconds and never feels like a to-do list.
