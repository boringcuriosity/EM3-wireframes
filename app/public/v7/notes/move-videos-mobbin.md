## Synthesis: patterns to try, ranked

All on a pure white page. Cards use a soft tinted surface only where text sits in a body. Corner radius 16 on cards, 12 on thumbnails inside cards, full pill on chips. Only the Move score carries the pillar colour.

### 1. Rail card, image clean, meta below (recommended)
From Fitbit Premium, Insight Timer, Open.
- Width about 72% of the screen so the next card peeks. Thumbnail 16:10, radius 16.
- On the thumbnail: duration pill bottom left ("▷ 6 min", white at 90% with dark text, 12px, radius full). Save heart top right in a 32px white circle. Nothing else.
- Below, 8px gap: title (2 lines max, 15 semibold), then one meta line in grey: "Beginner · No equipment". Level as a word, not a chip.
- Done state: a small green check replaces the duration pill.
- Why first: the calmest option, readable on white, and every field has one home.

### 2. Exercise of the week hero
From Apple Fitness+, Tonal, Tempo.
- Full width minus 16px gutters, 4:5 or 1:1, radius 20.
- On the image: a small blurred pill top left ("This week"), bottom scrim with the title (2 lines, 22 semibold), a meta line ("8 min · Beginner · Breathing") and one full-width pill CTA ("Start"). Save heart top right.
- One hero only, no carousel. Keeps it a single clear pick.

### 3. Section header with count and "See all"
From Fitbit, Calm, Alan, pliability.
- Row: section name (17 semibold) left, "See all" text button right (or a chevron on the title if space is tight). Optional one-line purpose under the title in grey ("Calm your body in 2 minutes").
- 24px between sections, 12px header to rail. Rails scroll edge to edge with 16px leading inset.

### 4. Square short-format card for Breathing
From Alan Breathing, Insight Timer second row.
- 1:1, about 40% width so 2.3 cards show. Duration pill top left, heart top right, title below on one line.
- A smaller size tells people these are quick, and breaks the rhythm between rails.

### 5. Continue row with time left
From Netflix, Disney+, Roku.
- Appears only when there is a partly watched video, directly under the hero.
- Same card as pattern 1 but with a 3px progress bar on the bottom edge of the thumbnail (green fill on a light grey track) and the meta line reading "4 min left".

### 6. Locked as "coming up", not paywall
From Peloton program, Alan path.
- Keep the thumbnail visible with a light white wash (not heavy blur). Small lock glyph plus a reason ("Opens next week") replaces the duration pill. No price, no big lock in the centre.
- Empty Saved: one icon, "Tap the heart on any video to keep it here", one secondary pill "Browse videos".
