The option chip, when a meal can span options

Today the chip carries two things: which option you are looking at, and a tick on the one you started. Once food can be logged from more than one option, that is not enough. The chip has to answer a third question at a glance: how much of this option is already in your day.

Six states, not two. Viewing is one axis and progress is the other, and they are independent: you can be looking at an option you have logged nothing from, or have half of an option in your day while reading a different one. Every variant below is shown across all six, because a chip that reads beautifully in two and falls apart in the other four is not a solution.

The sentence is gone. "You have started option 2, which is how your coach planned this meal" has been removed from the card. A paragraph that restates what a control already shows is a second copy of the same state, and it goes stale the moment the control can carry it.

What to judge each on. Can you find the started option without reading? Does viewing stay distinguishable from logged, given both want to be green? Does it survive three options on a 390px screen? And does the completed state feel like an end rather than just more of the same?
