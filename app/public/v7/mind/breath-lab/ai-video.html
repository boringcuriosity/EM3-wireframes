Breathing, as video

One 12 second clip made in Magnific (Kling 4.0, keyframes) of a whole breath. The clean breathing-in and breathing-out parts were cut out and given extra in-between frames, then each plays stretched to its exact time: 4 s in through the nose, 6 s out as a gentle sigh through softly parted lips. A hold is a still picture. No air is drawn and there is no glow.
