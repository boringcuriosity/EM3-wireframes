Breathing sounds, to compare

Two ways to give the breathing a sound. Cues follow the breath itself: each one starts as you begin to breathe in, hold, breathe out or hum, and lasts that whole part. Music plays softly underneath and does not follow the breath. Either can be on alone, or both together.

Tap the sound button on any phone to listen to it (one phone at a time). In the app, sound stays off until the person turns it on. The final visuals
