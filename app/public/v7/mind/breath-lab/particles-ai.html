A figure made of indigo dots

A refined take on the dotted figure, generated in Magnific (Nano Banana) for the three breaths that need a body to show them: Pursed Lip, Humming Bee and Belly. One gender-neutral person made of fine indigo dots joined by hairline lines, no hair and no clothing details beyond a plain tunic for the belly, sits inside the same breath clock with the same words and timings as final.html. Pursed Lip swaps between two aligned pictures, lips closed to breathe in and softly pursed to breathe out. Humming Bee is one picture, fingers closing the ears, cropped at the chest, rising a little on the breath in. Belly blends two aligned pictures, the belly rising a little under the hand. No air, mist or smoke is drawn.
