Every frame is the real Mind screen; tap a circle inside a frame to try it by hand, or use Replay. The score itself stays where it is: no option repeats it.

The cases every option handles

  • A tick (wind-down, breathing, me-time, mood): the gain, and what it came from, in one line.
  • An untick or a part already full: nothing new. The tick alone says it; there is never a minus or a +0%.
  • Two quick ticks: they add up in the one moment (+6%, then +13%), never two pills.
  • The task that finishes wind-down: the line says "your wind-down is complete", in place of the toast it used to show.
  • Mood check-in: "for checking in", since the answer never counts. Answered in its sheet, the gain sits just above the sheet, never over the reply.
  • Logging last night's sleep: the biggest jump of the day (often 40% to 50%). Only E gives it the full moment.
  • Reaching 100%: the line becomes "Your Rhythm is full for today". Crossing 80% still hangs the medal under the header, as before.
  • Reduced motion: no travel and no glow, the pill fades in still and out again.
