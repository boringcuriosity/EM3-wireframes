Mind · Rhythm · The moment a mark lands

The lotus, when something is done

When something is marked done, Mind's lotus opens in GoodFlip green, then the screen is exactly Momentum's win (Move's logged moment): the pill with a white tick naming what was done, the big +6%, and added to your Rhythm score. It is fast: the petals and the green land in about 0.4 s, the number counts up in 0.3 s, it holds about 1.1 s and fades out in 0.3 s. Every frame is the real Mind screen; tap a circle inside a frame to try it, or use Replay.

What every version does

  • Every gain: the lotus, then the pill ("Wind-down done", "Breathing done", "Me-time done", "Mood checked in", "Sleep logged"), "+x%", "added to your Rhythm score". Same at 100%.
  • An untick or a part already full: nothing. There is never a minus or a +0%.
  • Two quick gains: they add up on the one screen: "2 tasks done", +13%.
  • Mood check-in: shows once its sheet has closed.
  • Tap anywhere closes it at once; otherwise it is gone about 2.3 s after the tap.
  • Reduced motion: a still green screen with the gain, fading in and out.
