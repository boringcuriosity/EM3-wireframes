Does breathing feel too quick?

The coach found breathing too quick and suggested a 1 second rest between breathing in and out, and 2 seconds between breaths. We kept the 2 second rest after breathing out and left out the pause after breathing in. It is now in the app; below, the real player shows today’s timing beside the new one.

What I found

  1. The coach is right that it feels rushed. Today each phase hands straight to the next with no gap, so the moment you finish breathing out you are told to breathe in. Pursed Lip is the quickest: a full breath every 6 seconds, 10 a minute.
  2. The 2 second rest after breathing out is the natural one. At rest we all pause briefly after breathing out before the next breath starts. Giving that pause a place makes the pace feel calm without changing the exercise.
  3. The 1 second "rest" after breathing in is really a hold. With full lungs you can't relax, you can only hold, and a hold is a different technique. For people who get breathless (Pursed Lip is often given for that) a hold at the top can feel uncomfortable. I'd leave it out and make the turn at the top gentler instead, so the circle slows into it rather than stopping.
  4. 4-7-8 gets the rest too, Box does not. In 4-7-8 the rest comes after the long breath out, so its hold stays where it is. Box already pauses after breathing out (its second hold), so it stays as it is.
  5. Slower breaths mean fewer per session. The coach sets minutes, not breaths, so the session length stays the same with fewer breaths in it. At 4 a minute (coach's version of Extended Exhale) some people start to feel short of air; 5 to 6 a minute is the calm range most slow-breathing guidance aims for.

What changed in the app

  1. A "Rest" step after breathing out, 2 seconds, in Simple, Belly, Humming Bee, Extended Exhale, Pursed Lip and 4-7-8. Under the word: "Let the breath settle". In 4-7-8 it comes after the long breath out, so the hold stays where it is.
  2. No pause after breathing in. The picture already slows into the top of the breath, so the turn is gentle without a hold.
  3. The rest is quiet: the singing bowl rings on breathe in and breathe out only. No vibration either.
  4. The picture rests too. Pursed Lip has its own rest picture: lips closed and relaxed, no arrow. Humming Bee goes back to her quiet picture with no sound arcs. The belly film waits on its breathed-out frame, and the flower stays folded. On the breath clock the rest is a dotted arc, like a hold.
  5. The words say it: "In 5, out 5, rest 2" on the ready screen, a third step in How it works, and the coach portal's line for each exercise.
  6. Session minutes stay the same, so a session holds fewer, slower breaths.
