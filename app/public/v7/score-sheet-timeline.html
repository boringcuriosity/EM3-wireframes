Eat · Nutrition sufficiency

The score sheets, as a timeline

Every case of the line under the score, on a live phone, with the sheet behind its Learn more drawn as a timeline: a dot per meal, a plain sentence, its macros, and a quote card to close. Every sheet comes from one template, written out below. Tap Learn more inside a phone, or the link under it.

The template

Every sheet below is built by these rules from the log alone: the meals just logged, each with protein, carbs and fats eaten, its macro targets, and the points it added. Nothing is written per case.

1 · Title and kind line, from the overall change

Up, every meal perfectYour sufficiency has increased
You’re doing great. Keep following your coach’s plan and staying within your macros.
Up, a meal went overYour sufficiency has increased
Good progress! Sticking to the macros your coach planned for each meal will help your score grow even more.
Up, a meal was smallerYour sufficiency has increased
Good start! Eating the full meals your coach planned will help your score grow even more.
No changeYour sufficiency stayed the same
Try to keep each meal within your macros, and your score will improve a lot.
DownYour sufficiency has decreased
It happens sometimes, so don’t worry. Try to keep your macros in range for better nutrition.

2 · Each meal, one stop on the timeline

DotThe same indigo dot for every meal; the sentence and its tag say how it went
How it wentYour {meal} was perfect · was bigger than planned · was smaller than planned · had more {carbs and fats} than planned · had less {protein} than planned · had more {carbs} and less {protein} than planned
What it did(perfect) …and added +{x}% to your sufficiency score. · …so it only added +{x}%. · …so it didn’t change your sufficiency score. · …so it took −{x}% from your sufficiency score.
MacrosProtein, Carbs, Fats as {eaten} / {target}g, dark within target, amber more, grey less

A macro is within target at 80% to 120% of it: a placeholder that must match the score’s own rule.

3 · The last card

Last cardOne short line in a highlighted quote card, the same shape as the line under the score:
Overall, your score went up by +{x}%. · Overall, your score went up by only +{x}%. · Overall, your score did not change. · Overall, your score dropped by −{x}%.
One meal or manyThe same line: “Overall,” reads right for one meal, two, or the whole day.
PercentagesAlways a small tag with its sign: + in green, − in red.
