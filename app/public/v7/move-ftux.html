Meeting Momentum for the first time

The bottom sheet that opens over the Move screen the first time someone sees their Momentum score. It follows Eat's pick, Swipe through: what the score is, how the coach's plan grows it, and the medal and FlipCoins. Every phone is live: swipe it, tap through, close it and replay.

What it says, from move/momentum.js: out of 100 from Workout 45 (calories burned against the plan), Steps 25, Small moves 15 and moving in each part of the day 15. Going past a target adds no more, and a drop never shows a minus. What the coach switches off sits out, and the rest share the 100. 80 or more earns the Sneaker medal; a workout logged earns 8 FlipCoins a day. It starts fresh every day.
