Macro glyph stroke, every version

Only the hairline changes. The fill is held at the graphite you picked, and the card, the ground and the glyph artwork are the live ones from r3/eat.html. Each tile shows the card at real size, then the first glyph at 4x underneath, because a 0.5px hairline cannot be judged at 1x. The blown-up glyph is the same live element as the one in the card above it, not a copy.

The stroke is eight zero-blur drop shadows on .gwrap, one per compass point, because a CSS mask cannot take a stroke. So a variant can change three things: the colour, the offset (which reads as thickness), and how many shadows (four gives a softer, slightly broken edge on diagonals, eight gives a continuous one).
