Saying it is a rest day

A1 first, in five finishes, then a rest day with a workout logged, then the other options. On a rest day from workouts, often for recovery, the Workout tile reads No target with Rest day under it, a label in every option. Each option adds one way into the same sheet that explains a rest day in the simplest words. Every phone is the real Move screen: tap the rest day part to open the sheet.
