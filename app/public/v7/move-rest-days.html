When something isn’t planned today

In the coach portal, any day can be marked as a Rest Day for the workout, a No Moves Day for small moves, or a No Steps Day for steps. The user sees one phrase for all three: No target today. A workout can still be logged and steps still sync, so what’s done still shows; small moves only come from the coach, so with none there’s nothing to show. It says what the plan holds, not why, so it fits a recovery day, a cast or a wheelchair alike. A part with no target sits out of the score, and the parts left count for more, so 100% is still within reach. When all three are out, there’s no score that day.

How the score changes

The usual weights are workout 45, steps 25, small moves 15 and moving through the day 15. A part with no target drops out, and the others are scaled up in proportion to fill 100. Moving through the day comes from workouts and small moves, so when both are off it drops out too.

Rules

  • Logging on a day without a workout. A walk logged then adds to moving through the day, earns FlipCoins and shows the usual gain line. It never says “more than the coach’s workout” or “reached today’s workout goal”, because no workout was planned (checked in move/momentum-pick.js).
  • The medal is still earned at 80%, on the score from the parts left. A day with all three out has no medal.
  • Never a minus. Leaving a part out can make the score go up or down from the usual day. The user only ever sees the day’s own score, never a comparison.
  • Words for everyone. “No target today” for all three: it says what the plan holds, and still lets a logged workout or synced steps show. “Rest day” is the coach’s word in the portal, but on the user’s screen it would be wrong for someone whose steps are out for weeks after a cast, or for good in a wheelchair. These words describe the plan, never the body.

Not built yet

  • Trend and the week. A day with all three out should show as a day with nothing planned, never as a missed day, and shouldn’t break a streak.
  • KAIRA notes. The facts sent to KAIRA should say which parts are out, so a note never suggests something the coach left out.
  • Small moves only. With workout and steps both out, moving through the day is full only if small moves fall in all four parts of the day, or the user logs something in the gaps. The coach may want to place them that way.
