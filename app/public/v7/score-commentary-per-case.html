How each sheet is built
  • The title answers the line. Why your score went up, why it went down, a perfect day.
  • What happened: the meal against its targets, with the same numbers the line shows: what was possible, what was taken off, what it got. No meal names, no foods.
  • How it works: the one rule behind that move, in a sentence or two.
  • What to do next: always kind, always pointing at the next meal.

The general sheet with every rule in one place stays at score-commentary-cases.html, to compare.
