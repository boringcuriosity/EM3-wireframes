The score, in glass

A wash of colour down from the top of the screen, and behind the number itself a soft glass bubble. Both are live, and each one is sitting on the real plate rather than a picture of it, so the blur has the actual dots to refract and the bubble can be judged on what it does to them.

The wash belongs to the page, not to the plate: in the reference it starts under the status bar and dies out before the content, which is what makes it read as light in the room rather than a panel behind the score. All six share the same wash so only the bubble differs.
