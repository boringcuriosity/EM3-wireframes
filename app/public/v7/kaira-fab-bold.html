Kaira, the ambitious five

Not a bar sliding out from behind a button. In all five the launcher becomes something, or something leaves it and comes back. Each is built on a technique I pulled from award work or from a shipping app, named under each one. Real Eat screen, real launcher, injected live.

The one that changed my mind. Claude's own iOS listening state does not put a bar next to the button, it stretches the button into a capsule and puts the waveform inside it. The two circles flanking it are what the button split into. That is the whole transition, and it is better than anything I drew first.

What I had to verify before designing. An SVG goo filter only bridges two shapes across about one blur radius, so a launcher cannot "split in two and separate across the screen" and stay liquid. In A the mass therefore stays continuous: the capsule grows out of the disc and the two are welded the whole way.
Scroll a phone or wait to see each fire on its own.
