I have eaten something else, one line with a mark

Every card below is the shipping card: the same row with the helping under the name, the same kebab, and the coach's tips underneath, so each variant is judged where it actually lands rather than on a stripped mock. All of these keep the same words, the same place and the same 44px target. What changes is the mark.

The plus and Kaira are not the same promise. A plus says this adds a row and you do the typing. Kaira's mark says tell me and I will work it out. Putting both on one line asks the person to choose between two jobs before they know either is on offer.

The row has the tips below it. That is the real test. Anything with a container, a tint or a second line now sits between the coach's plan and the coach's tips, and has to not look like a third section of the card.

A label, then two named routes

The words stop being the button. I have eaten something else becomes the heading of a small group, and the two ways to do it are named outright: search and log it yourself, or tell Kaira. Nobody has to guess what a mark means, because both routes carry their own words.

This buys clarity with height. One line becomes three, on every meal card, every day, and now with the tips directly beneath it.

The two routes are not equal and should not look it. Searching is the road most people take, so it keeps the filled treatment. Kaira is the way out when you cannot name the food, the rarer case, so she sits quieter. Drawing them as identical twins is a false balance.
