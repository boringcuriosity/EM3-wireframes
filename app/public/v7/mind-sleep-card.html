The sleep card and its sources

From the simplest case to the hardest. Step 1 is Apple Health or typing it in. Step 2 adds the Arc smart ring, and step 3 has both. Every phone below is the real Mind screen; tap the three dots in any of them.

1. Choosing where sleep comes from

Everyone chooses a sleep data source once, even if Apple Health is already allowed (say from Move) or the ring is already paired. Until they choose, the card asks under it.

  1. Before they choose: the card says Add your last night's sleep with Log sleep and the three dots. Under the card: Choose your sleep data source.
  2. Tapping it, or Log sleep, opens Sleep data source with nothing selected. A connected device has a radio; one that is not connected has Connect. Once paired, the ring carries the green Recommended tag beside its name. Save works once one is picked.
  3. Manual picked: a line above Save says they can change it anytime from the three dots. The strip goes; they log each morning. If they came from Log sleep, Save takes them straight to it.
  4. Connect on Apple Health opens the Apple Health sheet, then Apple's own screen. Once allowed, Syncing for 5 s, then last night: From Apple Health.
  5. Anything they type says Manually added by you. Three dots, always: Edit your sleep (Log sleep when there is nothing yet), and Sleep data source.

On iPhone, Apple never tells an app whether reading sleep was refused; it just sends nothing. So "connected, nothing came in" also covers someone who said no on Apple's screen. Its words have to work for both: wait for it, or log it yourself.

When the ring and Apple Health disagree

Both are connected and, in the background, we compare them. When last night differs by 3 hours or more (say the ring was off to charge), the card asks which is right, in the gold striped strip with an info mark. Check opens a sheet to pick one, for last night only; the source stays as chosen. X keeps the night as it is.

2. Arc smart ring

Same card, with From Arc smart ring in place of Apple Health. Typed or edited times still win. An edit changes GoodFlip only; the Arc app keeps its own sleep score. Edit on a ring night first opens a Before you edit sheet that says so.

3. Ring and Apple Health

  • The ring is read first. When it missed a night and Apple Health has it, that night comes from Apple Health and the strip says Your ring had no data.
  • Ring lost or broken: they pick Apple Health in Sleep data source.

Open questions

  • Does the Arc ring pair inside GoodFlip, or do we read it through the Arc app?
  • If the Arc app also writes to Apple Health, the same night arrives twice. Rhythm should skip Apple Health entries that came from the Arc app.
  • 3.2 fills a missed ring night from Apple Health by itself. The other way is to leave it empty.
