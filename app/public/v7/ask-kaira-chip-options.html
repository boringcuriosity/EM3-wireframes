Ask Kaira, on each tip's own headline

A question happens about one tip, not about tips in general, so the chip rides on each tip's headline in the place the info circle used to hold. Seven surfaces for the same chip: same words, same geometry, same 44px target, shown once on its own and then on the real card. Breakfast carries two tips, so the repetition is visible.

It repeats, so weight is everything. One per tip, several per card, a dozen down a day. At that count anything with a fill, a shadow or a loop stops being a nice detail and becomes the texture of the screen. This is the test every variant below is really taking.

The green belongs to logging, the gradient belongs to Kaira. Every tick and every action on this card is GoodFlip green. Her mark is the one indigo to teal thing on the screen, which is why it is recognisable and why it must not outweigh the tip it is attached to. Hover the chips to see how each one moves.
