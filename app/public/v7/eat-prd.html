1Overview

Problem

Today's Diet page tells a person how many calories they ate. It never tells them whether they ate well.

  • The only number is calories consumed of a goal. Nothing measures the day against the coach's plan.
  • Ticking a plan food leaves the page for a separate form, then comes back.
  • After a log, nothing says whether it helped. Protein, carbs and fats live on a separate insights page.
  • Skipping a plan food and never logging it look the same to the coach.

So logging feels like admin. People log some meals, coaches see half days, and the plan never becomes a habit.

Why now

  • EM3 puts four pillar scores on Home. Eat needs a score of its own to sit there.
  • Coach plans already hold every meal's options and foods, so meal targets can be worked out from data that exists.
  • Snap and Voice logging already work through KAIRA.

Solution

  1. Log faster. Tick the coach's foods right on the meal card. Search, Snap and Voice for the rest.
  2. Score every log. Nutrition sufficiency, out of 100. Each meal adds its share when it matches the plan.
  3. Explain every move. One line under the score says what the last log did, and KAIRA explains why.
  4. Look back. Day history, a weekly Trend with KAIRA's insight, and Learn.

2Goals and success metrics

Goals

  1. Primary. More meals logged, on more days.
  2. Primary. Meals closer to the coach's plan.
  3. Secondary. People understand why their score moved.
  4. Secondary. Coaches see complete, honest days.

4The score: nutrition sufficiency

How closely each meal matched the coach's plan on protein, carbs and fats, added up for the day. Out of 100.

Set once, when the coach assigns the plan

  • Meal target: protein, carbs, fats per meal. The middle option's totals.
  • Meal share: meal calories ÷ day calories. All shares add to 100%.
  • Meals under 20 kcal (water, tea) carry no target and no share.

Worked out after every log

Eaten (x) against target (T)Macro points
Below 80% of Tx ÷ (0.8 × T) × 100
80% of T up to the top100
Above the top100 − (x − top) ÷ T × 100, lowest −50

Top = T + 20% of T, or T + 3 g, whichever is larger.

Meal score = average of its protein, carbs and fats points
Meal adds = meal share × meal score
Day score = sum of logged meals, rounded, shown no lower than 0

Example: breakfast is 21.3% of the day, target 13 g protein, 47 g carbs, 6 g fats

7KAIRA behaviour and evaluation

KAIRA writes the commentary answers and reads food from photos and voice.

Commentary answers

  • Must: use only the numbers from the score engine. Name the foods eaten. Compare to "the Eat plan suggested by your coach". End on what the next meal can add.
  • Must: stay within four short paragraphs, plain words, KAIRA in capitals, no em dashes.
  • Refuses: medical advice beyond the plan. Points to the coach.
  • Abstains: when the score data is missing. Says it cannot read the day yet.

Food from photo and voice

  • Must: put every recognised food in the tray with a quantity, for the user to check.
  • Abstains: when no food is found. One line, with search offered.

8Technical considerations

Constraints

  • Day boundary is the user's local day. Today the server's todays_date() ignores timezone.
  • Future days cannot be logged. Client rule; the API is unchanged.
  • Score is computed on the server so app, Trend and date sheet agree.

Integration points

  • calories_adherence_patient: grams and calories per day. Ranges 7D to 1Y for Trend.
  • KAIRA logging endpoints, including unknown food lookup.
  • Plan meal time windows, per meal division.
  • FlipCoins: diet is an activity type for rewards.
