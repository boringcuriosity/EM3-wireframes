The sufficiency badge

When today's Nutrition Sufficiency crosses 80%, the day earns one badge. It has no name. Always the same badge, no levels; it counts how many times it's been earned. The moment shows the badge, says why in one line, and adds the FlipCoins today's logging earned.

Six ways to stage it, each live. Press Replay under any phone to play it again.

Simple badges, in the HeartBadge style

Your HeartBadge, kept exactly: the soft hexagon, the lighter rim, the deep glowing centre, one outlined glyph. Only the glyph and the colours change. Each glyph in two frames: all green, and a gold rim around the green.

Sprout B, other shapes

The same gold rim, glow and sprout, in six frames. Only the outline changes.

The badge, round one

Generated in Gemini: chunky, glossy, shimmery, in GoodFlip's green and gold. The hero is a plate holding a bean, wheat and a drop of oil, the same three nutrients as the macro icons, with a sprout for growth. The other four are earlier concepts from the same session.

The moment, six ways

Every version carries the same content: a cheer, why it was earned, and +45 FlipCoins earned today. They differ in how the delight arrives. All motion eases in and out; nothing bounces.

The words

Name

  • No name. The badge is just "a badge". The sprout says it, the line explains why.
  • Names explored earlier and parked: Full Plate, Fuelled Right, Well Fed Day.

The sheet

  • Title: Yay! You earned a medal
  • Line: We've rewarded you with a medal because you gave your body what it needed today. Protein, carbs and fats all landed close to your coach's targets.
  • Coins: +45 FlipCoins earned today
  • Button: Share your achievement. The X closes it.

Other cases

  • First ever: "Yay! Your first medal". Line: "This is what a day matched to your coach's targets looks like."
  • Again: "Yay! Another medal", with "Earned 4 times" under the badge. Line: "That's your 4th one. Another day you reached your coach's targets for protein, carbs and fats."
  • A past day, logged late: "Yay! A medal for Sunday". Line: "Your late logs filled in the day. It counts all the same."
  • KAIRA, optional: "Days with enough protein keep you full for longer, so the evening snack craving tends to be quieter."
