The line under the score, meal by meal

The per-case sheets (score-commentary-per-case.html), but each one shows the meals behind that moment: protein, carbs and fats of every meal logged, against that meal’s macro targets set by the coach, and the points each meal added. People log one meal, two at once, or the whole day, so the cases cover all three.

How each sheet is built
  • The title answers the line. Why your score went up, why it went down, a perfect day.
  • 1, 2 or 6 meals logged: every meal in that log, as the same card, its protein, carbs and fats against its own targets, and the points it added. One meal, two at once, or the whole day.
  • How it works: the one rule behind that move, in a sentence or two.
  • What to do next: always kind, always pointing at the next meal.
