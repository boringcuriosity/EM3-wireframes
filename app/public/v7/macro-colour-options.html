Macro fill colour

The same card, glyphs and liquid fill as the Eat screen, at three levels: Protein part full, Carbs most of the way, Fats at target. One colour for all three, from the GoodFlip tokens only: every family on goodflip-design.vercel.app/foundations/colors is here (brand, indigo, success, teal, gold, warning, and gray for reference). Error red is left out, since a red fill can only read as wrong. Each family runs dark, normal and light: the fill moves from its deepest step at the base to a lighter step at the surface, and the empty part is that family's pale tints, so the vessel reads as one object filling up rather than a grey sticker.

Where the colour law points: the macro card reports logged food, which the brand deck files under indigo (action and tracking). Green belongs to the score above it and to primary actions, so a green fill repeats the score's colour. Both families are here so you can see the trade.
