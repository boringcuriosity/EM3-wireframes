01What Eat includes

Eat turns the coach's diet plan into a daily habit: log what you eat, see a score move, understand why it moved.

New logging experienceLog the coach's plan right on the meal card. Search, Snap and Voice for everything else.
Score against loggingNutrition sufficiency, out of 100. Every log moves it, meal by meal.
Commentary against the scoreOne line after every log says what that log did, with KAIRA one tap away to explain why.
Meal cards with the planEvery meal open, with the coach's options, foods, and tips.
Win momentA short celebration after every log, showing the gain.
Day historyStep back through days, or pick one from a month of score rings.
TrendThe week's score, each day, macros, and KAIRA's weekly insight.
LearnArticles, podcasts and infographics on eating well.
Coach plan checksThe coach portal sets meal targets and flags options that would score badly.

04The score: nutrition sufficiency

How well each meal matched the coach's plan on protein, carbs and fats, added up for the day. Out of 100.

Inputs, fixed when the coach assigns the plan

  • Meal target: protein, carbs, fats for each meal. The middle option's totals.
  • Meal share: meal's planned calories ÷ day's planned calories. Shares add to 100%.
  • Meals under 20 kcal (water, tea) have no target and are not scored.

Points per macro, per meal

Eaten (x) vs target (T)Points
Under 80% of Tx ÷ (0.8 × T) × 100
80% of T up to the top100, full marks
Past the top100 − (x − top) ÷ T × 100, lowest −50

Top = T + 20% of T, or T + 3 g, whichever is more.

Meal score = (protein points + carbs points + fats points) ÷ 3
Meal adds = meal share × meal score
Day score = sum of every meal logged, rounded, never below 0

Example: breakfast, 21.3% of the day, target 13 g P · 47 g C · 6 g F

What was eatenP · C · F pointsMeal scoreAdds
One option, as planned100 · 100 · 100100+21.3
Half a portion67 · 69 · 6266+14.1
Two whole options31 · 20 · 6739+8.3
All seven options−50 · −50 · −50−50−10.6

Rules

  • A meal counts once anything is logged in it, or a plan food is skipped. Until then it adds 0.
  • Foods from any option and off-plan foods all add up into one plate for that meal.
  • The score is worked out again after every log, from the whole plate.
  • The meal a food counts toward is decided by its log time.
  • Starts at 0 each day. The score is green. Nothing else on the screen carries the pillar colour.

The KAIRA answer

  • Opens KAIRA's chat with the question already asked.
  • Answer, in order: what happened, the foods eaten by name (extras tagged), resulting macros against the Eat plan, the rule in one line, what the next meal can add.
  • Ends on a forward step. "Keep logging honestly."

Hidden when there is no score: before a plan, free user, a day with nothing logged.

07Meal cards

  • Always open. Plan order.
  • Header: meal name, "Suggested 8:00 - 10:00 AM · 486 of 382 cal", + button.
  • "YOUR COACH'S PLAN": option chips, check on the chosen one. Food rows: tick circle, name, quantity, calories, ⋮ menu.
  • Food logged outside the plan sits above the plan with a "Manual" tag. Free users see no tag.
  • "TIPS FROM YOUR COACH": non-food plan items, each with a tick and a one-line tip.

A plan food has three states

StateLooksCounts
Not yetEmpty tick circleNothing
LoggedFilled tickIts macros, at the logged quantity
SkippedName struck through, stays on the plan0 eaten. The meal counts.

⋮ menu: change quantity, skip, remove from your day. "Put it back on the list" undoes a skip. A coach's food is never deleted.

08Logging the plan on the card

  1. Tick a plan food. It lifts off the row into a floating bar at the bottom.
  2. Tick more, from any meal. The bar shows the foods, "Edit quantity & time", Log, and X.
  3. Each food has its own time. Default: now if its meal window is open, else the middle of the window.
  4. While a meal has foods in the bar, its other options are locked. One option per meal.
  5. Tap Log. Win moment plays, then the day returns with the foods ticked and the score stepping up.

Edge cases

  • Time still to come (dinner ticked at 1 PM): Log opens the review sheet. Late times turn amber, "Set them to now" is one tap, Log waits until every time has passed.
  • Time wheel: future times greyed, Done waits.
  • X on the bar: slides aside to reveal Remove. Clears the bar.
  • A tick is never a silent log. Quantity and time are always visible before Log.

10Win moment

Plays after every log, then hands back to the day view.

  1. The Log button sweeps primary green across the screen, bottom right to top left.
  2. Three things only: the "Breakfast logged" pill, the big gain "+n%", and "added to your sufficiency score".
  3. Clears itself. The day view shows the score stepping up and the commentary line.
  • No plan or free user: the gain is replaced by what went in (calories).
  • Past day: the pill names the day.
  • Gain of 0 or less: the pill and "Logged". The commentary line explains.
  • Motion: smooth ease, no bounce. Haptic on the sweep.

15Coach portal: plan checks

  1. Coach picks a template.
  2. Portal sets each meal's target (middle option totals).
  3. Every option is scored against its meal target with the same points rule.
  4. Red dot on an option if any macro, eaten exactly, scores under 60. Shown on the meal tab, the option tab and the number.
  5. Assign is blocked until every red option is fixed with food or disabled.
  6. On assign, the patient gets meal targets, day goals (meals added up) and meal shares.

17Rules everywhere

Bottom sheets

  • Title top left. Filled circle X top right, 44px target. No drag handle.
  • Max 90% height. Header stays, body scrolls. Backdrop 50% black, slide up 300ms.

Visual

  • Page background pure white. Cards: subtle border, soft shadow.
  • Green is the score and the primary action. Indigo is KAIRA. Gold is insight and reward. Teal is a calm accent.
  • Macros are one green, told apart by their own icons.
  • Motion: smooth eases, no bounce. Respect reduced motion.

Copy

  • Plain, friendly, second person. British spelling. No em dashes.
  • KAIRA in capitals. She gives insight, never a recap. Two short sentences max.
  • No meal counts ("2 of 6"). Point to the next log instead.
  • Text 12px and up. Tap targets 44px and up.

18Open questions

  • Junk can score well when its macros happen to fit a meal (junk-score-answers.html). Add a food quality check?
  • Water in a food meal: picking jeera water over haldi milk scores 0 for that meal.
  • Meal targets can add up to more than the calorie goal (1,466 vs 1,200 on GLP).
  • Small targets are touchy: a 1.2 g gap can cost half the protein points.
  • How far back a person may log.
  • Swipe down to dismiss a sheet.
