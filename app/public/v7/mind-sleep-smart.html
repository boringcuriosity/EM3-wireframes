How it decides

  1. The ring first. If the Arc smart ring is paired, last night comes from it. It syncs inside GoodFlip, so Sync Now is real.
  2. Then Apple Health. If Apple Health is allowed, last night comes from it. If the ring missed a night and Apple Health has it, Apple Health fills it in and the strip says so.
  3. Else they log it. Typed or edited times always win for that night, and say Manually added by you.
  4. Asked once. The first time on Mind, the Apple Health sheet asks once. X or I'll log it myself means they log it; we do not ask again. Connecting later lives in the three dots, under Track sleep from.

On iPhone, Apple never tells an app whether reading sleep was refused; it sends nothing. So "nothing came" also covers someone who said no on Apple's screen, and its words must work for both.

3. The strip under the card

Always tappable; every part of it opens Track sleep from. Once a device is connected, the time sits at the left with the source's icon and Manage at the right end.

  • Nothing connected, nothing logged: Track sleep automatically.
  • Syncing: Syncing with Apple Health (or your Arc ring), no action until it is done.
  • Synced: Last synced 7:22 AM, Manage.
  • Synced, nothing came: No sleep data found · 7:44 AM, Manage.
  • Logged by hand, nothing connected: Added by you, 7:12 AM, Connect.

4. More filled states

Dismissing a gold card

Tap X (or Yes on the short night). The card rises and softly blurs as if sliding back under the strip above, then the space closes smoothly.

5. Track your sleep automatically

From the strip or the three dots. No box: two rows on the sheet. Not connected: Connect Arc smart ring, Reads sleep while you wear it; Connect Apple Health, Reads sleep from other apps or devices; each with Connect. Connected: its name, a green dot with Last synced, and Sync.

6. Log as questions

GoodFlip's quiz card. A pick goes green and the card folds into its own small card: the question, the answer in bold, and Edit. No opens the wheel inside the card. At the end both cards stay, with You slept for 7h 30m over Save Sleep.

Free users

No coach, so no suggested times: the wheel is the question. When did you sleep? then When did you wake up?, each with Done, then You slept for 7h 30m and Save Sleep.

7. Another sleep

Most people sleep once, so it stays out of the way: a row in the card's three dots, once a night is saved. It opens the sheet with When did you sleep again? and When did you wake up again?, each with Remove and Save.
