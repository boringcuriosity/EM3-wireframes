Every KAIRA note

Each phone logs one thing and opens the note by itself, as the patient sees it after tapping Learn more: a shimmer first, then the note. Until the AI is live the app shows its own note in the same format the prompt asks for.

The prompt

One system prompt for every Rhythm note. The app sends only facts (what was just logged, the score before and now, last night against the plan, what is still open and what each would add). The AI never works out a number. Read from the live screen (MDKAIRA.PROMPT in mind/rhythm-say.js).
