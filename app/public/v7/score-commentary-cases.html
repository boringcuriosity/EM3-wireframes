How your score works

A fresh start every day

Your score starts at 0% each morning and grows as you log your meals. When every meal meets your coach’s suggested targets, you reach 100%.

When it goes up

When a meal meets your coach’s suggested targets for it, it adds everything it can to your score.

ExampleBreakfast just as planned adds +23%.

When it goes up just a little

Eat a little less than your coach’s target, and the meal still helps, just a bit less.

Eat a lot more than the target, and your score is reduced, so the meal adds less than it could have.

ExampleHalf your breakfast adds about +12%. A double breakfast adds only +5%, when it could have added +23%.

When it comes down

Sometimes a meal turns out much bigger than your coach’s targets, and your score dips. That’s alright once in a while, it happens to all of us. Let’s try to stick to your coach’s targets to avoid losing points.

ExampleA large pizza at lunch brings your score down by 14%.

Days worth celebrating

Reach 80% or more and you’re a champion: today’s medal is yours. Try to get there on most days, it’s how you reach your goals.

What the sheet answers
  • How does it work? It starts fresh each morning; meals that meet the coach’s targets add the most.
  • Why did it go up? A meal that meets its targets adds all it can.
  • Why only a little? Eating more than the coach suggested loses points; staying within the targets earns them all.
  • Why did it drop? A much bigger meal dips it, said gently, with a nudge back to the targets.
  • What is worth aiming for? 80% or more: a champion’s day and a medal.
