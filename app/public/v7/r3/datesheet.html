The date sheet

The chip at the top opens a month of days, each carrying its own sufficiency ring. Tap a day to move the page to it.

Zoom

Rules the sheet holds to

  • Today is the last day anyone can reach. Future days are greyed and the forward arrows stop at today.
  • Days before the person joined are greyed too, and the month cannot step back past the month they joined.
  • A ring is a score. A dashed ring is a day nothing was logged on, and it is still tappable.
  • A dot is a day logged before the coach set the plan, so it has no score to show.
  • A full ring with a centre dot is a day every target was met.
