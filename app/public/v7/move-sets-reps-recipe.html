Input: the plan, nothing else

setsHow many rounds
reps or holdTimes to do it, or seconds to hold it
sideOne side, or each side
restSet · restNextSeconds between rounds · before the next exercise

The lines it builds

DoDo it {reps} times · Do it {reps} more times
HoldHold it for {hold} seconds · Hold it again for {hold} seconds
Each side+ on each side
RestRest for {restSet} seconds
LastTake {restNext} seconds, then start the next exercise

The order and the words

OrderDo, rest, do, rest… do, then the last step. No rest after the final round.
WordsAlways “it”, never the exercise name, so nothing ever breaks.
