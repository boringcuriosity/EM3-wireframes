The meal card, measured and respaced

The same card twice. Left is exactly what ships, every rule copied out of r3/eat.html unchanged. Right is the same markup on a six-value scale. Every number in the table below is read off the live boxes with getBoundingClientRect rather than typed in, so neither column can drift from what it actually renders.

The rule underneath all of this, from M3: "Define padding and gaps on the parent container to organize all elements inside" and "Avoid defining margins on child elements as they usually aren't uniform, and require more tokens." SPEC.md line 38 already says the same thing.

And the one for headings, from USWDS: "Use at least 1.5 times the amount of whitespace above the heading as below it." Carbon ships 2:1. The three uppercase labels in this card are currently 1:1, 2:1 and 0.75:1.
