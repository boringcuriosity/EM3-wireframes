Meal card buttons, on the system

Under I have eaten something else every planned meal card carries two ways in: Search and log and Log with Kaira. Today neither is a GoodFlip button. Each card below is the shipping card with only those two changed, so they can be judged where they live.

What the system gives us. Five button types and no others: primary, secondary, error, and the Kaira pair. The type decides the colour. The Kaira gradient is reserved for handing work to Kaira and must not be borrowed for an ordinary action. Flat (no lip) exists for rows too tight for the press travel. One primary per screen. Every meal card repeats this row, so whatever goes here appears four to six times on one screen.
