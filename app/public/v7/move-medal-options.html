The Move medal

Momentum is Move's score for the day: a workout is worth 40, steps 10, small moves 10, and moving in each part of the day 10 more, four times over. When today reaches 80% or more, the day earns a medal.

Every Move medal is built exactly like Eat's: the same gold rosette edge, the same gold rim and the same Flip green centre. Only the emblem in the centre changes, so the two read as one set. Ten emblems follow, each shown four ways: large, hanging on the Move screen, small in the calendar, and in the celebration sheet. Press Replay to play one again.

The shell comes from Eat's medal. Rosette, rim, enamel, bevel, shadows and the raised glyph with its mint fill and white line are copied part for part. Move only draws a new centre.

The idea to carry: moving once is a start, moving often is the goal. The best emblem says "you kept moving" at a glance, at 28 pixels as well as at 200.

When the medal is earned and shown

The rule

  1. 80% or more. A day earns the medal when its Momentum reaches 80% or more.
  2. One a day. A day has one medal at most. It stays the same medal every time, with no levels.
  3. It stays. Once earned, the day keeps its medal, even if a log is later edited.

What happens

  1. The first time today crosses 80%, the celebration sheet plays once.
  2. After it closes, the medal hangs on Eat's gold and green ribbon under the header for the rest of the day. A tap on it opens the sheet again.
  3. Past days show the medal in the calendar, in place of that day's ring.

The words

  • Title: Yay! You earned a medal
  • Line: Congratulations, you received a medal for keeping your body moving through the day. Your workout, steps and small moves all added up.
  • Coins: +45 FlipCoins earned today, as on Eat's medal sheet.
  • Button: Awesome! Keep Going. The X closes it.
