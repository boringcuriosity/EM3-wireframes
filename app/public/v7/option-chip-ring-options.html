The ring, four ways

D had two faults and both are fixable. The 1 was written twice, once in the ring and once in the label, and the ring kept showing a number after it was full, which wastes the one moment it has something to celebrate. In all four below the completed ring becomes a solid disc with a tick in it, and none of them says the ordinal twice.

The trade being made. Dropping the word buys a row that fits three options easily and puts progress on the thing that names the option. It spends the word "Option", which is the only thing telling a first time reader what these chips are. A and D pay that price in different ways: A never says it, D says it only on the one you are reading.

Each row is shown at 390px with all three chips, under the dashed frame, because a chip that is elegant alone can still make a row that wraps.
