    The line under the score

    One fixed sentence under the score; only the % changes. Before last night is in, it asks for it; after that, it says to keep going. After a log the score steps up first, then the line names what was logged and the gain in Momentum's green capsule (“Your breathing added +10%.”), one line per kind: sleep, wind-down, breathing, me-time, mood. Every gain line ends in Learn more, and a tap anywhere on the card opens KAIRA analysis: a shimmer while KAIRA writes, then one sentence on why the score moved and one or two next steps from what is still open today, with what each adds. The AI is not live yet, so the sheet shows a sample built from the same facts the AI will get; the prompt and the request JSON are in mind/rhythm-say.js (MDKAIRA) and in coach-mind/mind-plan-defaults.md. The nudges before a gain have no Learn more, as on Move. &kaira=1 opens the sheet by itself once the line lands.

    Sleep window: how it is judged

    The designer's question: if I slept as the coach planned and took a small nap in the afternoon, does the nap take my sleep window to zero? Until 16 Nov it could halve it, if the nap reached the score as a sleep: every session was added up as hours, and the window read the first bedtime and the last wake-up of all of them. From 16 Nov the window judges only the night (the main sleep), and naps add to the hours.

    • Which sleep is today's. Every sleep that ended between 6 PM yesterday and 6 PM today. Overlapping or repeated sessions (a watch and a phone) are merged, so no minute counts twice. Pieces under 10 minutes are dropped.
    • One night, not pieces. Sleep with a break of 1 hour or less is one sleep. A break that sits wholly inside the coach's sleep window (awake in the night) keeps it one sleep up to 3 hours.
    • The night is the longest sleep. The sleep with the most time asleep is the night. Every other sleep is a nap.
    • Naps count towards hours, never the window. Decided 16 Nov. Nap time from the same 6 PM to 6 PM span adds to the night's hours; the window still reads the night alone. The card says so: “Also a 20 min nap at 2:00 PM. It counts towards your hours, not your sleep window.” Oversleep works as before, so a long nap that takes the day more than 1 h over the target costs hours points (a 90 min nap after a full night: 25 to 23).
    • A night needs 3 hours. A hard cut, decided 16 Nov: under 3 hours asleep, the window gives 0 and the card says “Under 3 hours of sleep, so your sleep window gets no points today.”
    • Sleep consistency (the window), 25 points. Bedtime (the night's first sleep) and wake-up (its last) earn half each, 12.5: full within 30 min of the plan, then a straight line down to 0 at 2 hours off, early or late alike. Minutes off d: 1 if d ≤ 30, 1 − (d − 30) / 90 up to 120, 0 after. 1 h late keeps two thirds of its half. The patient sees the two edges in words and one total, “Sleep consistency: 21 of 25”, never 12.5.
    • Sleep duration (hours), 25 points. Time asleep in the night plus any naps, breaks left out. Full at the coach's hours up to 1 h over, 0 at 3 h short, a straight line between; past 1 h over, 20% less per extra hour.
    • Night-shift sleepers. Decided 16 Nov: the coach sets one sleep window in the day, for example 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, and it is judged exactly as a night window. The coach portal's bar turns to midnight to midnight for a day window, the patient's card draws 6 AM to 8 PM, and the portal's preview scores the same numbers as this screen. The portal's second sleep window is not needed and stays parked (?split=1).
    • Where it is explained. On the screen, the Rhythm sheet shows the sleep points. There is no separate sleep-points sheet and no nap card: naps are counted quietly (decided 16 Nov 2026).

    Section

    Who sees what

    Three kinds of people open Mind. Free users (no GoodFlip care plan) have no Rhythm score at all: no score bubbles, no line under them, no plan and no check-ins. They see their sleep from Health and can do any of the seven breathing exercises. People waiting for a Mind plan see the score locked, with the line saying it unlocks after their first Mind Coach consultation, their sleep, and the breathing exercises. Plan users get the full screen and breathe from their plan, so the "Breathing exercises" section is hidden for them. Breathing done from the section never changes Rhythm.

    Section

    Mood check-in

    One question, five faces. Picking a face logs it at once, the same for any face: a quote card (the commentary's quote mark, no KAIRA name) shimmers in with a supportive line, and one Done button closes the sheet. Each check-in opens 3 hours before the coach's time and stays open until midnight; tapped earlier, a sheet says when it opens. The row reads Mood check-in, "How are you feeling today?" and the time, with no chevron. (Before: Thank You, KAIRA after Awful or Low, Got It otherwise). There is no Log button. Another face replaces it, and the first time is kept.

    Breathing

    Tapping a breathing row opens the full-screen player, with the exercise's final picture (the seven in breath-lab/final.html). Tapping its circle, not yet done, opens a sheet named for the exercise: Start breathing, or I've already done it. The sound button sits left of Start from the ready screen on. The length sheet ends with No time limit, which counts up from 0:00. One minute breathed ticks the row in the background, right then, even if they keep going; the saved minutes are the whole minutes breathed. X or End asks: at 1 minute or more "N min saved" (Save and exit, Keep breathing), under it "Stop breathing?" (Keep breathing, Leave). A done row, tapped on its body or its circle, opens Eat's item menu: Breathe again, Mark as not done.
