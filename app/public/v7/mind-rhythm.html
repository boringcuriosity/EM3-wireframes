    Your mind plan

    The coach's plan in parts of the day, each item in the part its time falls in; an empty part is hidden. Every row is ticked or unticked, with the time the coach set on the right: before the chevron on rows that open something (breathing, mood), plain on wind-down and me-time. A breathing row shows only its minutes, "3 min".

    Section

    Mood check-in

    One question, five faces. A pick is a draft: KAIRA's card shimmers in, then replies. Only Log Mood logs and scores it, the same for any face. After that only the face can change, and the first time is kept.

    The check-in

    Low and very low

    FaceKAIRA replies
    😣 Awful"I'm sorry today has been this hard, and thank you for telling me. You don't have to fix it all at once. When you're ready, five minutes of slow breathing settles your body first, and the mind often follows."
    😕 Low"Thank you for being honest with me. Low days come to everyone, and they do pass. Something small you enjoy, a song, a short walk or a call with someone you love, can lift a low day sooner than waiting it out."
    😐 Okay"Okay is a fair answer. Most days are like this, and they still tell us a lot over a few weeks."
    🙂 Good"Good to hear. What made today good? Noticing it makes it easier to have more days like this."
    😄 Great"That's lovely to hear. Hold on to what went right today, it's worth doing again."

    The Rhythm sheet

    Still to update. Tapping the score opens this sheet. Its words are Mind's, but its header is still Move's: "49% Momentum", tiles that read Sleep 3 of 7.5 h and Wind-down 2 of 3 on every day, and "Your Move coach". It should show the table below.
    PartPointsHow it counts
    Sleep hours23Full at the coach's hours, up to an hour over. Falls to 0 at 3 h short; past an hour over, loses 20% an hour. A split night counts time asleep only.
    Sleep window24Bedtime and wake-up, half each. Full within 30 min of the plan, 0 at 2 h off, early or late alike. A split night reads the first bedtime and the last wake-up.
    Wind-down23Each task ticked earns its share.
    Breathing10Each exercise done in full earns its share.
    Me-time10The activity done earns all 10.
    Mood10Checking in counts; the face never does. With two check-ins, each earns 5.
    • Sleep is 70, self-care 30. The coach sets the plan; the weights stay fixed.
    • Only what the plan has counts. A part the coach left empty (no wind-down, no breathing or no me-time) is left out of the points and the total, so every plan can reach 100.
    • The day starts at 0, and last night counts once it is in.
    • The header should read today's Rhythm, the three tiles from the screen, and the Mind coach.

    Open questions

    1. Should saved breathing minutes count before the plan is reached?

    A 3 min plan done as 2 min, saved, scores nothing until the last minute, though the player says "Minutes add up through the day".

    RecommendedA. Count minutes pro rata2 of 3 min earns two thirds of that exercise's share.Catch: points rise while the row stays unticked.
    B. Keep it all or nothingAs built: the row and the score move together.Catch: "Minutes add up" shows nowhere until the end.
    C. Any minute or more counts in fullSimple and generous.Catch: the coach's minutes stop meaning anything.

    2. The templates leave me-time blank

    Left blank, the patient sees no me-time row and self-care leans on breathing and mood alone.

    RecommendedA. Assign waits for an activityThe coach portal asks for it before Assign.Catch: one more required field.
    B. A general row when blank"Do one thing you enjoy".Catch: less personal.
    C. Leave it emptyAs built: the score rescales over the parts left.Catch: less self-care in the day.

    3. Past days show today's plan and today's night

    Only the mood row knows it is a past day.

    RecommendedA. Read the day's own recordThat day's plan, night and ticks, with mood still addable.Catch: needs per-day records from the backend.
    B. A read-only summaryThe score, the tiles and the mood.Catch: past mood still needs its row.
