    Apple Health and Health Connect

    Where sleep comes from on Android, decided 17 Nov 2026: by hand, from Health Connect, or from the Arc smart ring through Health Connect. Without the ring there are only two choices, Log sleep or Turn on Health Connect. With the ring we still ask for Health Connect: allowed, the ring writes its sleep there and we read it; not allowed, sleep is typed in. With both, the strip under the card reads “From Arc ring via Health Connect” (the ring icon is a placeholder until it arrives). Every case, its copy and live frames: mind-sleep-sources.html.

    Sleep sources Manual, Health Connect and the Arc ring 21 cases: no ring, with the ring, first time and returning. What the card, the strip and the sheets say in each. Open the doc

    iPhone, from 19 Nov 2026: no source to choose. GoodFlip decides. The Arc smart ring first if it is paired (it syncs inside GoodFlip, so Sync is real). Then Apple Health if it is allowed; if the ring missed a night and Apple Health has it, Apple Health fills it in and the strip says so. Else they log it. Typed or edited times always win for that night and say Added by you. The first time on Mind, the Apple Health sheet asks once; X or I'll log it myself means they log it, and we do not ask again. Connecting later lives in the strip and the three dots. Apple never tells an app that reading sleep was refused, so “nothing came” also covers someone who said no, and its words work for both. Every option: mind-sleep-smart.html.

    The line under the score

    One fixed sentence under the score; only the % changes. Before last night is in, it asks for it; after that, it says to keep going. After a log the score steps up first, then the line names what was logged and the gain in Momentum's green capsule (“Your breathing added +10%.”), one line per kind: sleep, wind-down, breathing, me-time, mood. Every gain line ends in Learn more, and a tap anywhere on the card opens KAIRA analysis: a shimmer while KAIRA writes, then one sentence on why the score moved and one or two next steps from what is still open today, with what each adds. The AI is not live yet, so the sheet shows a sample built from the same facts the AI will get; the prompt and the request JSON are in mind/rhythm-say.js (MDKAIRA) and in coach-mind/mind-plan-defaults.md. The nudges before a gain have no Learn more, as on Move. &kaira=1 opens the sheet by itself once the line lands.

    What shaped your score?

    On by default from 17 Nov 2026, between the line under the score and Last night, as on Eat and Move. Two tiles in Move's indigo glass, Sleep quality and Self-care, each with an ⓘ, each shown as its own score out of 100: 49 of a possible 70 for sleep reads “70% of 100%”. The 70/30 weights and each part's share stay inside the Rhythm and are never shown to the person (the dev weights are in The Rhythm sheet). There is no strip under the tiles. Free users and people waiting for a plan have no score, so the card is hidden for them.

    • Tapping a tile opens a sheet that only explains, with no bars and no numbers for today: “Sleep quality shapes most of your Rhythm. A good night sets up everything after it: your mood, your focus and how much energy you have.” (Self-care: “Self-care makes up the rest of your Rhythm.”) Then each part with one line on what it is and a soft “For a good score” box, for example “Aim for about 7h 30m of sleep, the amount your coach set for you.” Got it is pinned at the bottom.
    • The options explored, with the strip, the tile layouts and the gold or indigo strip: mind/research/score-parts.html.

    Who sees what

    Three kinds of people open Mind. Free users (no GoodFlip care plan) have no Rhythm score at all: no score bubbles, no line under them, no What shaped your score?, no plan and no check-ins. Their sleep card reads “Your last night”, 24px under the date, and the seven breathing exercises follow as full cards. People waiting for a Mind plan see the score locked, with the line saying it unlocks after their first Mind Coach consultation, their sleep, and the breathing exercises. Plan users get the full screen; after their plan, one slim row, “More breathing exercises · Additional exercises to try”, opens all seven on their own screen. Only the plan's own breathing counts for Rhythm.

    The coach sees the patient's Rhythm in the portal: the Rhythm overview (the night against the window and each day's self-care, a tap on a day shows what was done; sleep sources are not shown) and the Mind plan, where the weights read Sleep 70 pts (duration 25, consistency 25, wind-down 20) and self-care 30 (10 each), each scoring rule sits behind “How it is scored”, Assign offers Assign to the patient only, Update the master template (named, with who created it) and assign, or Save as a new template and assign, and templates show who created them. Both work on a phone.

    Your mind plan

    The coach's plan in parts of the day, each item in the part its time falls in; an empty part is hidden. Each card's subtitle is only its times, “6 AM to 12 PM”, Night “9 PM to 10:30 PM” (the plan's bedtime), with no “x of y done”. Every row is ticked or unticked, with the time the coach set alone on the right, so the times line up. A row that opens a sheet, breathing and mood check-in, carries its chevron right after the title, and the whole row opens it except the tick. The title's first line is centred on the tick. A breathing row shows only its minutes, “5 min”.

    Rhythm breakdown. Under each part's title, the chip Move and Eat use (Movement breakdown, Nutrition breakdown). It opens a sheet for that part of the day: “Suggested 6 AM to 12 PM · what you did, against this part of the day's plan”, then one row per standard part in it (Last night's sleep, Breathing, Me-time, Mood check-in, Wind-down tasks) with its icon, “x of y” against “Target: y” and Eat's bar with the count inside, then “What counts” with one plain line per part shown. No task list and no scores. Tap the chip on any card in the frames below.

    Mood check-in

    One question, five faces. Picking a face logs it at once, the same for any face: a quote card (the commentary's quote mark, no KAIRA name) shimmers in with a supportive line, and one Done button closes the sheet. Each check-in opens 3 hours before the coach's time and stays open until midnight; tapped earlier, a sheet says when it opens. The row reads Mood check-in ›, "How are you feeling today?" and the time; the whole row opens the sheet. (Before: Thank You, KAIRA after Awful or Low, Got It otherwise). There is no Log button. Another face replaces it, and the first time is kept.

    🙂 Good"Good to hear. What made today good? Noticing it makes it easier to have more days like this."
    😄 Great"That's lovely to hear. Hold on to what went right today, it's worth doing again."
