Less on the Log sleep sheet

Today the sheet shows seven things at once: the coach's plan, a bar with its legend, two time tiles, an open time wheel, Add another sleep with its line, the total, and Save. Most mornings the person only needs two times. Each option below is the real sheet; tap the times in it.

What changed in every option: the time cards lift off the sheet with a soft shadow and a chevron, so they read as tappable; the chevron turns green and points up while its wheel is open. The moon is indigo and the sun gold, so bedtime and waking are told apart at a glance. The coach's plan sits in a soft indigo chip rather than a line of grey text. The line under Add another sleep goes.

Why B: the times are the answer, so they lead; the wheel only appears for the one time being changed. E is B with the result first, good if the hours matter more than the times. D is fastest on an ordinary night but adds a step on any other. F suits people who think of the night as a stretch, but dragging is harder to get exact.
