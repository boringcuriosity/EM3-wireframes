Times still to come

At 1 PM someone ticks dinner and bedtime foods and taps Log. The sheet has to say, kindly, that those times haven't happened yet, offer Set them to now as GoodFlip's secondary button, and keep Log waiting. Six ways to do it. Tap Set them to now in any phone to see the sheet settle.

Every colour is a GoodFlip token and every icon comes from the GoodFlip Figma. No orange anywhere: later is a time of day, not an error.

The sheet, six ways

Same foods, same moment (now 1:00 PM). What changes is the note, where it sits, and how a later time looks on its chip. Switch to a mixed day to see each one when lunch is eaten and dinner is still ahead.

Scenario

How a later time looks

The shipped chip is filled amber, which reads as a problem on every row. Each of these keeps the chip a value you can tap, and lets the difference be shape or tone instead of alarm. Left is a time that has passed, right is one still to come.

The words

Generic, so the same words work for any meal at any hour. It counts items, not times: four foods at two times read as "4 times".
