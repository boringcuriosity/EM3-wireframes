The bigger the gap, the bigger the thing we ask about.

Home asks about a row: have you had your lunch. That is the right size of question for somebody who was here an hour ago. For somebody who was last here on Tuesday it is the wrong size, because it asks about an hour when what is missing is a week. Five people, one rule: the unit of the ask grows with the gap.

KAIRA asks, in the language they think in

Tap a reply and see where it goes. Hinglish is scoped to this one conversation, in Roman script, so the rest of the interface stays as it is.

KAIRAIn your coach chat

Each reply buys something

  • I will log daily asks for a reminder time on the spot, because a promise with no time attached is gone by evening.
  • Busy these days drops the week to one ask. Later it will genuinely shrink the plan and hold the streak.
  • Something is getting in the way asks which pillar is hardest, and takes more than one answer. It is the only place the product learns why somebody stopped.
Where the answer goes

Whichever pillars they pick land in the coach portal against that person, before their next consultation. The coach opens the call already knowing that Move is the part going wrong, and the promise the chat makes, that the plan changes at the next consultation, is one the coach can actually keep.

Why KAIRA says it

She sends it herself, in the coach chat, and her opening line is what earns her the right to: she says what the missing days cost her own ability to help, "baaki dinon ka andaza lagana padta hai". That is insight about the gap. She keeps it that way and leaves "please log" to the button underneath, so she stays the voice worth reading.

04

Four things to settle before wireframes

Each one changes what gets drawn.

1. Does the passive user get "welcome back" too?

Welcome back tells a three-day-a-week person that they were gone, and it works against the sheet they just read.

Recommendation: passive keeps its bubbles and the gap sheet. Welcome back starts at inactive.

2. When exactly do the bubbles merge?

Too early and the morning arrival loses their four bubbles. Too late and the evening logger has already worked through the rows by hand.

Recommendation: after 7 PM with three or more pillars empty, matching the night phase the day model already uses.

3. Sheet and chat message in the same session?

Two interruptions on one return usually means the second one is dismissed without being read.

Recommendation: the sheet opens, the chat waits behind a badge.

4. How is an archetype worked out?

A stored label goes stale the moment the person changes.

Recommendation: days logged in the last 7 and 14, computed in one place, with a Control Panel chip for every state.

What ships when

Everything above is a message, and messages can ship on their own. Two items here are behaviour and wait on the day model holding more than today: "Busy these days" genuinely shrinking the week, and the streak surviving a week somebody declared quiet.

GoodFlip EM3 wireframes. Nothing here is built yet. Once the four questions above are settled, each scenario becomes a state in the Control Panel so the whole set can be walked and checked before it reaches anybody.
