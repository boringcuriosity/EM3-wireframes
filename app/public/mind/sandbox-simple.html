Start from

Sleep

How long, and when. One window: at night, or in the day for a night-shift sleeper.
Fixed by the app, the same for everyone: sleep duration is full at the target and falls to zero 3 hours short; more than 1 hour over loses 20% an hour. Sleep consistency gives full marks within 30 minutes of each time, falling to none at 2 hours, judged on the main sleep only, and none if it is under 3 hours. Naps add to the hours, never to the window.

Wind-down tasks

Each task done earns its share.

Breathing

Up to 3 of the final seven. Each one done earns its share; one counts after a minute of breathing.

Me-time

One activity. Doing it earns all of me-time. Leave it out and breathing and mood share its points.

Mood check-ins

One or two a day. Checking in counts; the face picked never changes the score. With two, each earns half.
