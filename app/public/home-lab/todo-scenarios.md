# Today: the full to-do, scenarios first

Today shows the whole day's plan as one to-do in four parts: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night.
Ticking a task drops it into the floating bar at the bottom (as Eat's plan logging already does), so
several things can be logged at once.

## 1. What a task can be (from the v7 pillars)

| Pillar | Task | Where it comes from | How it is done |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eat | Each planned meal (breakfast, mid-morning, lunch, evening snack, dinner, bedtime), with its foods | Coach's diet plan, meal times | Tick foods, or Snap / Voice / Search for something else |
| Move | Each routine (exercises with sets, kcal) by part of the day | Coach's exercise plan | Play the routine, or tick it done |
| Move | Small moves (up to 5, no time) | Coach's plan | Tick |
| Move | Steps goal (10,000) | Coach's plan | Counts itself from phone, ring or typed; never ticked |
| Mind | Last night's sleep | Coach's sleep window | Ring, then Apple Health, then typed (smart sleep card) |
| Mind | Breathing exercises (1 to 3, timed) | Coach's Mind plan | Opens the player; counts after 1 minute |
| Mind | Me-time (one, timed) | Coach's Mind plan | Tick |
| Mind | Mood check-in (1 or 2: morning 7 AM, evening 9 PM) | Coach's Mind plan | Pick a face |
| Mind | Wind-down tasks (up to 6, from 9:30 PM) | Coach's Mind plan | Tick |
| Measure | Device syncs (BCA weigh-in, CGM sensor, ring charge) | Devices owned | Opens the device |
| Program | Consultation, lab test, prescription | Care program | Opens it |

## 2. Who is looking (sidebar "Who is this")

1. Program, all three plans assigned (the full day)
2. Program, some plans assigned (one or two pillars have tasks, the others wait for the coach)
3. Program, no plans yet (only first steps: book consultation, labs)
4. Each device adds or changes rows: BCA adds a weigh-in, CGM fills glucose after meals, Ring fills sleep and steps by itself
5. Device-only and free users: no Today tab (decided earlier), so not in scope here

## 3. Moments in the day

1. First open of the day, nothing done
2. Mid-day: morning done, partly done, or skipped
3. A part of the day just ended with tasks left
4. Everything in a part done (the part folds)
5. Whole day done
6. Late night, tasks left from the day
7. A rest day (Move turned off for a part or the whole day)
8. Coach changed the plan today

## 4. Interactions

1. Tick one task, tick several across pillars, log them together from the bar
2. Untick before logging (bar removes it), remove from the bar
3. A task whose time is still ahead (Future time, as Eat does)
4. Tasks that cannot be ticked (steps, sleep from a ring): they show progress instead
5. Skip a task
6. Open a task's detail (meal foods, routine, breathing player)
