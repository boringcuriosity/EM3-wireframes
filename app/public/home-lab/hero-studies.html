Recommendation

E. Up now. It is not a new component: it is Eat's own meal card (scene, pictogram, Playfair title) and the list's own tick, lifted to the top. The hero and the list are visibly the same object, so ticking one ticks the other, and lunch as planned is 2 taps. The real scene art gives it warmth without H's height, and when you scroll past, it follows as a one-line strip, so the question is never lost. If it feels too plain, the closest runner-up is H. How the question is picked

  1. A meal that ended in the last four hours and was never logged is asked about once, gently ("Did you manage to eat lunch?"). "I skipped it" is a real answer and is never asked again.
  2. Then whatever is open right now, the one that closes soonest first. A meal at 1 to 3 PM beats a small move that runs all afternoon.
  3. Then the next thing coming. If it is more than 45 minutes away, it says you're caught up and names the next one, rather than asking early.
  4. Older tasks left open (a morning workout at 6 PM) are not chased by the hero. They stay in the list as "still open".
  5. "Not yet" and "Later" move on to the next question for this visit. The task stays open in the list.
  6. Nothing left: a short goodnight. No counts, no recap.
  7. No plan: the hero is the first coach consultation. Free user: logging a meal.

Answers that log

  • Mood: the five faces sit in the hero. One tap logs it; the reply carries a reason or a next step, and a low mood points at a practice.
  • Simple ticks (me-time, last caffeine, wind-down, small moves): Done logs it from the hero, the row ticks below. F adds an Undo.
  • Meals: an option, "As planned" or the tick lands that option's foods in the bar. One more tap on Log, so the member still sees what is logged.
  • Workouts and breathing: Start glides to the routine; the player is the pillar's own.

Open questions for the designer

  1. E shows the task twice, in the hero and in the list. Tag the list row "Up now" (shown), dim it, or hide it?
  2. F logs simple ticks at once with an Undo. Keep that, or send every answer through the bar like meals?
  3. E's strip under the tabs when you scroll past: keep it, given the floating bar also lives at the bottom?
  4. Scenes (E, H): Eat has one per meal, Move and Mind four each, so a picture can repeat across a part of the day. Enough, or should Figma add a welcome scene (no plan) and an all-done scene? For now H uses the early morning family scene and the night reading scene.
  5. I's ring: should it step up after a log? That needs a real per-task gain from the score engine; it stays still here, to stay honest.
  6. Free users: keep the Free moment, or drop it (earlier decision: no Today tab for free)?
  7. Missed meals: ask only about the last one within four hours (shown), or every missed meal in turn?
  8. Does "Not yet" snooze for this visit only (shown), or until the next part of the day?
  9. K and L show sample history ("twice this week", "up 6 on last week", "Day 18"). Is there a real source for these, and what does K say in the first week, before any pattern exists?
