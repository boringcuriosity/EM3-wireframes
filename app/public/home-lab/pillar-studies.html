How the states work, in every variant

With a plan
The score, in the pillar's colour: Sufficiency 54%, Momentum 40%, Rhythm 49%. The only place the pillar colour is spent.
Before a plan
Same frame, neutral grey, a padlock or a dash where the number goes. The words say when it unlocks, short on the tile and in full on the detail page.
Free user
No score and no padlock, because there is nothing to unlock and no coach to mention. The tile carries a plain fact they did earn (942 kcal, 5,080 steps, 7h 20m of sleep) or a first thing to do.
Mixed
Each pillar decides for itself. Eat scored with Move and Mind locked reads as one row, because the frame never changes. When all three are locked, the row says it once.

Recommendation: E, next gain

It is the only variant that answers both halves of the job without a voice: where do I stand (the score) and what moves it next (one fixed line, "Lunch adds up to 28%"). The line is a template where only the task and the number change, so it never recaps and never drifts into vague words. The frame holds every state: the same line slot says "Unlocks after your consultation" before a plan and invites a first log for a free user.

Runner up: G, medal line. Every pillar already gives a medal at 80%, so a finish line on the tile is a goal the member already knows. It could later fold into E as a thin bar above the gain line.
