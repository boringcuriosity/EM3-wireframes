Meal card, four options

All four keep the layout, the content and the interactions exactly as they are. Only the dressing changes: colour, gradient, border, depth and the add control. Each column shows three meals in three different states, because the real test is what happens when the card repeats six to eight times down a scroll.

Drawn from GoodFlip's own repeating card stack on the ring Home page, node 5834:34325, where three of the six cards are one component instanced three times. That is the only true precedent in the Figma files for a list like ours.
