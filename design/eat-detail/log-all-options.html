Log all, five options

The shortcut that logs every remaining planned food in a meal at once. Today it is a full width button at the bottom of the card. It appears on up to six cards down one scroll, which is the whole problem: the design system says a repeating card should not contain a button at all.

Each column shows two meals so the repetition is visible. Options 1 to 4 come from a Mobbin survey of nutrition diaries, grocery lists and checklists, where every app that repeats a meal card puts its per-meal control in the card header row, right aligned, as a pill or an icon, never a bar. Option 5 is what GoodFlip itself does.

One correction worth knowing: the action should be green, not indigo. Indigo never labels an action anywhere in the GoodFlip corpus; where it appears as text it is always a data value or a content tag. Indigo belongs on the card edge and the score, green on the verb. Option 1's tint is shown as drawn, but its label would move to green.
